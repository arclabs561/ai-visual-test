When you donate to the FSF as an associate member, you support the essential freedoms for all computer users, with a sustaining contribution. Associate membership is $120 annually (or $10 per month). For students, the rate is just $60 (or $5 per month).

Over 80% of our funding comes from individuals — your contribution matters! Join with over 5,000 active members in 86 countries, and all 50 US states representing a diverse membership of computer users, artists, software engineers, hackers, students, and activists.

Why become an associate member?

When you donate as an associate member, you are part of an informed society working together to make a better world: respectful of individual freedom, social solidarity, personal privacy, and democracy — built on free software.

Benefits of your membership level donation include:

  • USB Membership card loaded with GNU/Linux
  • 5% off free software-friendly devices from ThinkPenguin
  • Five member.fsf.org email aliases for forwarding
  • 20% discount on all items in the FSF shop
  • The biannual FSF Bulletin delivered to your home
  • A member.fsf.org XMPP Jabber account for instant messaging
  • Access to our exclusive members forum
  • Access to freedom-respecting videoconferencing via our Jitsi Meet instance

Plus you support our work on the GNU Project and campaigns for free software adoption and development.

The Free Software Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, so your member donation is tax-deductible in the US.

How we use your donation

Charity Navigator has awarded the FSF its highest possible rating of 100% for our commitment to fiscal responsibility and transparency. We work hard to keep costs low so we can continue fighting for essential freedoms for computer users.

Additional ways to give

There are other ways to donate, too: you can donate cars or stocks, or give through the United Way.

We prioritize your privacy and do not sell or distribute our contact lists. Read our full privacy policy.
