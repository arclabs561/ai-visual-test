Contact Steph

Please note: I do not accept requests for guest blog entries, advertising, or reviews. Requests for any of those through this form will not be answered.

For all other messages: I have to apologize for just how overloaded my inbox is right now. In this year of the pandemic, with kids at home and long covid in our household, I have very little non-parenting time and energy. The time and energy I still have, after parenting commitments, simply has to be focused on my fiction-writing so that my books will still get written. I hope you can understand.

I do try to catch up on my messages whenever I can, but I’m also aware that some are slipping through the cracks just because I’m overwhelmed right now. If I don’t reply within a month (and if your request is not about any of the topics listed above), please do feel free to re-send your message.
