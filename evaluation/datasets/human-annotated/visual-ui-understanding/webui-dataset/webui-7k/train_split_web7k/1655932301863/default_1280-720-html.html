Catacombs

John Farris

synopsis

John Farris’s tribute to H. Rider Haggard and King Solomon’s Mines is an action-packed thriller with international espionage, rare, powerful jewels and a lost race of cat people. With a new introduction by David J. Schow and a new afterword by the author.
      Originally published in 1982, this understated classic again shows John Farris, a master of plotting and characterization, at his finest, and, again, going in a totally capricious direction. Signed by Farris, Schow, and artist Harry O. Morris, and limited to just 300 copies.
