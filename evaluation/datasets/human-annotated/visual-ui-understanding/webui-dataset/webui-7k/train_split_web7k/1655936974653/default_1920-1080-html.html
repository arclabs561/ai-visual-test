For Libraries

WordFire Press is pleased to offer a variety of plans and discounts to individual, system, public, and private libraries across the United States. These plans, representing a variety of genres from notable authors, offer not only a substantial discount, but will provide your patrons with desirable stories that circulate well.

Our plans are based on a short, one-year commitment, that will save you money, but also because we’re certain that you’ll find our titles an excellent addition to your collection. All of our books are produced in a sturdy, hardcover edition with a dust jacket.

We’re fans of our authors, but we’re also big fans of libraries, knowing that they serve as a valuable community resource for people of all ages. We’re excited to be a part of your library or library system and invite you to contact us at any time you have questions!
