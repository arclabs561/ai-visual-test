Releasing Day EP (Music)

My new EP with 3 tracks.

May, 2020

tl;dr; Check out Day on Spotify, and other places to find my music on my website.

The first (and last) time I created music to learn what the new streaming music business process was like, was over 3 years ago.

Then, I launched an EP titled "eDreams" (formatted to "E Dreams" on most platforms, blergh), with 4 instrumentals: 2 electronic and 2 rock/metal.

Today, I'm launching a new EP, titled "Day", with 3 instrumentals, all electronic, where I mostly arranged some loops, effects, and recorded a few instruments like piano, bass, and guitar.

I only own an electric guitar, but GarageBand and an effects pedal do wonders!

To publish the music to streaming services, I used EmuBands again; the process was straightforward and easy.

There's also a list of other places where you can get my music in my website now (linked on top).

I hope you enjoy it.

Thank you so much for being here. I really appreciate you!

by Bruno Bernardino
