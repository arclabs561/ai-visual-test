Exhibits/Collaborations
I enjoy collaborating with other artists or collectives and I'm eager to participate in more exhibits. If you'd like to work with me, please get in touch!

The Club of Odd Volumes

I was part of the Odd Collective at The Club of Odd Volumes for a year in their 2014/2015 artist rotation.

Supergraph 2014


I exhibited and sold my work in the 2014 Supergraph event from Feb 14-16 2014 at the Royal Exhibition Building, Melbourne. I exhibited three limited edition A4 giclee art prints. The Pineapple Float Image was chosen as The Design Kids top picks in the Supergraph Newspaper.

Supergraph 2015


I participated in Supergraph again in 2015 at the Royal Exhibition Building in Melbourne Feb 13-15. I exhibited a limited edition A3 giclee art print titled 'La Plage'.

Pick Me Up London


My work was selected by Supergraph to be exhibited at the Pick Me Up graphic art fair in London in 2015. I really wish I could have seen all the amazing work in person!
