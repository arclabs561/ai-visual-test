    COASTS. COMMUNITIES. CONSERVATION.

    Founded in February 2017, we are a marine & coastal conservation based social enterprise, headquartered in North Goa. We are working towards empowering the local community through our responsible nature based experiences, conservation travel itineraries and integrated environmental education programmes conducted through community partnerships.

    As a social enterprise, our mission is to maximize environmental and social impact. Through our travel experiences, we support our social impact work that focuses on creating awareness about conservation issues, building capacity of various stakeholders at the grassroots level for sustainable livelihoods and conservation action.
