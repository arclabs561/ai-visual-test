Pharo Services

Pharo is a purely object-oriented programming language that provides a powerful environment focused on delivering simplicity and immediate feedback. Pharo’s stabile yet lean core system and excellent development tools make it a cutting-edge platform for developing for mission-critical applications.

We help our clients develop new products and business models and provide expert advise and support when it comes to restructuring and migrating their existing IT infrastructure in Pharo. Whether it’s customized software solutions, web-based applications or smart, connected products and IoT applications, we’re here to design and implement single-source solutions in Pharo from start to finish no matter your particular project.

We’ll walk you through every phase and aspect working in the Pharo development environment entails – no matter the scope of the project, for clients both large and small. To be sure no one’s left in the dark once we’ve implemented your solution, we also offer workshops to bring affected employees up to speed. We’ll handle all installation, maintenance and testing related tasks in person wherever you’re located and will work closely with stakeholders in your team to design the optimal solution for whatever project you envision.

For us, Pharo is a pioneering technology for software of the highest quality. As part of the worldwide Pharo community, we’re instrumental in driving development forward and have both the deep knowledge and wide-reaching experience it takes to use Pharo as a powerful development tool.
