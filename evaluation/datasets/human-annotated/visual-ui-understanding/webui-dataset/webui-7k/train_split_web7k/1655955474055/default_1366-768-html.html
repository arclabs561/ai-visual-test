So what exactly is masonry?

Ever wondered what Vancouver masons of brick and stone do?

If you’ve come this far, then you may have a general idea of what a mason is. The reality is that there is so much more to our work. Masonry is the task of building structures from basic blocks.

 

The blocks are then bound together by mortar, which completes a finished structure. To put it simply, a mason build a structure by using smaller objects, which include:


  • Bricks
  • Stones
  • Marble
  • Granite
  • Limestone
  • Cast Stone
  • Concrete Blocks
  • Glass Blocks
  • Adobe



By assembling any of these objects into a shape, the mason ties everything together with mortar, which is a cement-like paste that hardens and keeps the shape of the structure.

 

Masons are oftentimes called bricklayers, but the entirety of their work is designing and building an entire structure, rather than just laying bricks.

Different types of masonry

Masonry is an extensive trade. If you have come here looking for help from a Brick mason Vancouver but aren’t sure of what you want, here are all of the different types of masonry services you can request for your home or business. Please note, our Vancouver masonry company specializes in brick masonry, stone masonry, and block masonry.

Stone Masonry

Stone is the most weather-resistant, strong, and durable construction material available. Depending on its location, stone structures can last for more than 1,000 years. As a result, stone is the most used type of material in masonry construction. 

Brick Masonry

Bricks are created using burnt clay blocks. Brick structures perform very well, but the number of openings, size, and position can affect the overall strength of the structure. 

Block Masonry

Solid concrete blocks are a more economically and environment-friendly alternative to bricks. Larger than bricks, it’s much easier to install concrete blocks into a single structure. 

Veneer Masonry

This is a favorite choice for home renovation projects because they provide ideal insulation and economy for many homeowners. Depending on your preference, it can be placed to cover an entire wall or support a wood frame. 

Gabion Masonry

Gabions are composed of galvanized steel to look like baskets of fractured stone. These materials are able to drain water, snow, and frost. ﻿

Composite Masonry

This is the construction of a structure that is composed of many different types of stones.

The Pros and Cons of Hiring a Mason

If you’re on the lookout for skilled Vancouver masonry contractors, then it’s important to weigh the advantages and consequences of making your decision. Can you just do the work yourself?

 

Will your finished structure be safe from inclement weather? How long will it last? These are all questions you should have answers to before moving forward with your decision. To help you make an informed decision, here are the pros and cons of hiring one of the leading masonry companies in Vancouver:

PROS

  • When used, bricks and stones can improve the thermal mass of a building, which enables it to store heat longer and more efficiently.
  • Masonry products are non-combustible, which means that your property can be protected from fire.
  • Walls, fences, and other structures made from bricks and stones are naturally resistant to projectiles caused by tornadoes and hurricanes.
  • Masonry has a long shelf-life and can last for a millennium. 

CONS

  • Under specific circumstances, extreme weather can damage masonry. When water seeps into cracks of stone and freezes, it can damage the stone over time.
  • Masonry is extremely heavy and must be set on a strong foundation to avoid cracking.
  • Masonry offers far less imagination than wood framing. You can do much more with shaping and designing wood-based projects, as it requires a very specialized expertise to shape stone. 

There are a lot of different things to consider if you want to move forward with hiring a mason for your home or business project. If the pros outweigh the cons in your circumstance, then you can rely on the team at Vancouver Masonry to deliver impeccable stone masonry services for your property.
