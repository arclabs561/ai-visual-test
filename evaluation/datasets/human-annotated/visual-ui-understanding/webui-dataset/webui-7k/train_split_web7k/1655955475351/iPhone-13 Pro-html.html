BARRACUDA DIVING INDIA began operations in 1993 to promote the sport of recreational diving in India. Our membership, recognizes the highest standards of diver education, staff training, safety, equipment and dive services and denotes a quality control assurance to divers worldwide. The Barracuda team have proudly received many 'Awards of Excellence' for outstanding dedication to diver safety, education and environmental conservation. We also offer other International Diving Certification.

We specialise in offering a full range of SSI Certification dive courses, scuba diving equipment and dive travel services to destinations which include Goa, Karnataka, Maldives, Andamans and Lakshadweep. With 30 years of teaching experience. We offer - specialities, dive professional courses as well as Instructor Training courses.

Owners Venkatesh Charloo (India) and Karen Gregory (U.K.) are both highly qualified SSI Dive Control Specialist and React Right Instructors with extensive diving and teaching experience in Hong Kong, Philippines, Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Maldives, Andaman and Lakshadweep Islands. With 16 years of teaching experience, diving and travel in India we look forward to sharing our incomparable passion for the knowledge and beauty of our amazing underwater world.
