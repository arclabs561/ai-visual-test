POETRY

The Terraformer of Bigotry

by Dawn Vogel

​If only it was as simple

as building a device,

programming in the right constraints,

picking a suitable location,

and letting the terraformer do its thing,

to wipe out bigotry.

Erase it from the terrain,

skimming the sticks and stones,

to make a viable field.

Shape the climate

to remove the occurrence

of screaming storms like tantrums.

Creating a place

where all can live

together in comfort.

​

END

APRIL 2021
