What is Coolest Projects?

Coolest Projects is the world’s leading technology showcase for young people. It's a unique opportunity to get creative, have fun, and celebrate ingenuity.

Projects are showcased in the gallery for people all over the world to see! Coolest Projects Global is free and open to creators up to the age of 18, wherever they are in the world.
