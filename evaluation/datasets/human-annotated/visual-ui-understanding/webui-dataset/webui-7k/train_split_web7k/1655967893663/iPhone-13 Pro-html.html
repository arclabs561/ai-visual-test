THE TIMELINE OF GOD

Everyone has a desire to know what their future holds.

The fact of the matter is you are living in the future right now. Truly someone in years gone by desired to know what it would be like to live in this day and age. So, you are living in what was their future. There is a source that has provided people with accurate predictions of the future and many parallels as to how things would, are, and will unfold. That source is the Bible. The Timeline of God reviews how the Bible has within it a written record of things that have already come to pass, things that are happening right now and amazing things that are going to happen in the future. One of the most incredible things that you will discover is how structured and planned it all has been, is, and will be.
