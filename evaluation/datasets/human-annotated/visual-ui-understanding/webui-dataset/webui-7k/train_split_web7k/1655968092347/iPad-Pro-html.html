Ailuropoda melanoleuca

Hello and welcome to Ailuropoda Melanoleuca, a fanlisting dedicated to the Giant Pandas. These animals are endangered; there are about 2,000 of them left. While still very low, the numbers has increased since 1970 which is great. The pandas are often seen in a relaxed sitting posture eating bamboo in the mountains in China (and some zoos). You can find more information about them by going here. This fanlisting is listed at TheFanlistings.org under the Animals category. If you love these big bears, please grab a code and join! Navigation is at the bottom of this page. If you have any questions/comments, feel free to contact me. - Emelie

Ailuropoda Melanoleuca was opened on May 25, 2015 and was last updated on 21st June 2022. The fanlisting has a total of 45 (+0 pending) listed fans from 13 countries. Newest fan(s) is Marley.

→ What's a fanlisting?

A fanlisting is simply an online listing of fans of a subject, such as a TV show, actor, or musician, that is created by an individual and open for fans from around the world to join. There are no costs, and the only requirements to join a fanlisting are your name and country. - TheFanlistings.org
