Analytical cookies

Analytical cookies collect information about how a user uses the website, identify the most frequently visited sections, including the content that the visitor chooses when browsing the website. The information is used for analytical purposes to determine the interests of the site visitor to establish what could improve the site's functionality and make it more user-friendly. Analytical cookies only identify the user's device, but do not reveal the user's identity. In some cases, some of the analytical cookies are managed by data processors (operators) on behalf of the site owner in accordance with the owner’s instructions and only for the specified purposes.

Targeted (advertisement) cookies

Targeted (advertisement) cookies are also called marketing and targeting cookies. These cookies are placed on the website in collaboration with the University of Latvia (UL) marketing, advertising and targeting service providers. UL may use cookies and similar technologies from marketing partners, third party sites and social media platforms such as YouTube. These technologies help UL to measure the effectiveness of marketing and information campaigns and to understand how visitors reach UL websites from UL advertisement. UL uses these technologies to compile statistics about visitors who interact with UL websites and other UL online content to measure the effectiveness of UL advertisements and provide more relevant information to visitors. If a visitor does not allow the use of these cookies, he/she will be shown less customized ads. These cookies are stored on the visitor's device.
