About this shop

My name is Shilin, and this is a shop of my works in print! I work full time as a self-published artist, and spend most of my time working on CARCIPHONA and AMONGST US, as well as the occasional digital painting. Some create in order to leave behind a legacy, others to attain true mastery; me, I create and tell my stories simply out of compulsion, like I can't live without getting these things off my chest. So I run this shop in order to continue to create that which is dear to me independently, without worrying about stuff like appeasing publishers or sacrifice work quality just to make deadlines.

Thank you for helping me continue this journey! I hope you will find your glimpse into my world as exciting as I find my pursuit.
