About ICCMB

6th     International Conference on Computers in Management and Business

Warm welcome to the official website of the 2023 6th International Conference on Computers in Management and Business (ICCMB2023). The Conference will be held in Macau, China during January 13-15, 2023. The main purpose of ICCMB2023 is to provide an international platform for presenting and publishing the latest scientific research outcomes related to the topics of Computers in Business and Management. This conference offers good opportunities for the delegates to exchange new ideas, and to establish research and/or business links, as well as to build global partnership for potential collaboration. We sincerely hope that the conference will help advance knowledge in relevant scientific and academic fields.
ICCMB 2023 is co-sponsored by SAISE, University of Macau; assisted by National University of Singapore, HOSEI University and University of Applied Sciences and Arts Northwestern Switzerland.

Publication

Each submission will be reviewed by 2-3 reviewers. All accepted papers after proper registration and presentation, will be published in the ICCMB 2023 conference Proceedings.

Submission

Please log in the Electronic Submission System; ( .pdf only) to submit your full paper and abstract.
For any inquiry about the conference, please feel free to contact us at: iccmb2018@yeah.net .
a. Submit Full Paper:
We provide a good opportunity by admiring your updated research knowledge and also by publishing it in the conference proceedings of ICCMB 2022, which will be indexed by Ei Compendex, Scopus, and others.
Accepted full paper will be invited to give the oral presentation at the conference and be published in the conference proceeding.
b. Submit Abstract:
Accepted abstract will be invited to give the oral presentation or poster presentation at the conference, the abstract will not be published.
