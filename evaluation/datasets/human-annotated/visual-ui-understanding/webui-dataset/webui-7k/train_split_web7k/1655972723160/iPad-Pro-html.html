Our VAT Experience

When approaching us with VAT problems, you can be assured of our expertise:

 

  • Collectively we have nearly 50 years experience in VAT, including working for H M Revenue & Customs
  • We have worked with numerous businesses and charities, across many different sectors
  • We frequently work with Small and Medium Enterprises, providing cost-effective VAT advice and solutions
  • Written on the subject of VAT for some of the largest taxation publishers
  • Les also serves as a Member of the Tax Tribunal Service.

 

Savings & Successes

Not every vat consultancy case we deal with results in a VAT saving, but many do. Here is just a sample of our successes:

  • October 2018 – Assistance with Sports organisation to recover around £40,000 in VAT. As a postscript, avoided exposure to a further £100,000.
  • August 2018 – £60,000 saved on domestic housing development.
  • August 2018 – £150,000 protected on ‘annex’ to nineteenth century Church building.
  • June 2018 – £30,000 saved on modest residential development, utilising the reduced VAT rate.
  • February 2018 – education centre; £75,000 saved on construction project.
  • February 2018 – independent takeaway; assessment reduced from £150,000 to £40,000.
  • June 2017 – Approx £25,000 saved on construction project for international charity.
