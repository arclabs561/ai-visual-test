 Essays


If They Hang You I’ll Always Remember You
In 1999, the Mystery Guild published a series of reprints honoring their selection of the fifty greatest crime writers of the twentieth century. The Guild commissioned RC to write the introduction for Dashiell Hammett’s brilliant and classic crime novel, The Maltese Falcon.

Archie and Me
RC penned this introduction to a reprint edition of Rex Stout’s Before Midnight. In it, he discusses not only Stout’s classic creations, Nero Wolfe and Archie Goodwin, but also his own Elvis Cole and Joe Pike.

Wayne Warga is Alive and Well at 6000 Feet
This appreciation was written upon the death of RC’s close friend, the writer and journalist Wayne Warga. It was originally published in the newsletter of the Southern California Chapter of the Mystery Writers of America.

Where I Live
This essay for the Los Angeles Times describes why RC enjoys living in the Santa Monica mountains. It was originally published in the Times’ ‘Home’ section and generated an enormous reader response.
