Accessibility

PLEASE NOTE: the link to scooter rentals will be live closer to the convention. Thank you.

Accessibility services can be found on-site at Loscon, next to the Registration Table. Anyone wishing to have accessibility assistance should check in with them and obtain a ribbon to designate access. You do not need to wear the ribbon to obtain access services, but please have it available.

Disabled attendees with visual and/or hearing-related impairments will be seated by our Accessibility Services department as close to the front of the room as possible. We also reserve seating areas for other disabled attendees including those with wheelchairs – and our Accessibility Services team will assist you in any way possible to help you access these seats – but we will not guarantee seating near the front to anyone other than for visual and/or hearing impairment issues.

Contact Access services for directions to Accessibility Lines that include some chairs and early access to any evening events.

Loscon panelists are at floor level, so those with accessibility issues do not need to be concerned about access to risers or stages at any panels.

If you have accessibility issues not addressed by this post, please contact our Access committee at: access@loscon.org This link will be made live later in 2022. Please check back then.
