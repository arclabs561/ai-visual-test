Privacy Policy

How we use Cookies
Cookies are very small text files that are stored on your computer when you visit some websites.

We use cookies to help identify your computer so we can

  • tailor your user experience.
  • access anonymous analytical information about your use of the site.

You can disable any cookies already stored on your computer, but these may stop our website from functioning properly.

The following improves user experience in the operation of our website.

This web site will

  • remember your preferences, for example font, font size, etc.
  • remember your last-used search engine on the Textise home page.

The following helps us to recognise usage trends (anonymously).

This web site will

  • use Google Analytics to provide Information about your use of our site including details of your visits such as pages viewed and the resources that you access. Such information includes traffic data, location data and other communication data.

This web site will not

  • share any personal information with third parties.
