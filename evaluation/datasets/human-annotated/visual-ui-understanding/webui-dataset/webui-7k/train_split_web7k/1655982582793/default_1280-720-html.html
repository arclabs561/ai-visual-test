How can I read newspapers and magazines with Pressreader?

  • 3 months ago
  • Updated

Visit pressreader.com and sign in with your library card number to download newspapers and magazines.

  1. Click Sign in
  2. Select Library or Group
  3. Find your library service (the first 3 letters should work e.g. Dub / Tip / Wex)
  4. Enter your library card number and click Log in.
  5. Start reading newspapers and magazines.

There are also apps available on Google Play - Android | iTunes - iOS | Kindle Fire.

 

The Irish Examiner is available when you access PressReader using the Wi-Fi in any public library and you can retain access by using the app. This means that for the next 5 days you can continue to access new editions of The Irish Examiner without needing to go back to the library.

 

You don't need to log in when in a library, but you may choose to do so to see your favourite publications and saved material.
