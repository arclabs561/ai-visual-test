Victorians. A Journal of Culture and Literature

Victorians: A Journal of Culture and Literature welcomes interdisciplinary approaches to Victorian literature and culture that respond to developmental shifts in the discipline of Victorian studies. Formerly named The Victorian Newsletter (ISSN: 0042-5192), Victorians Journal looks forward to continuing its long history of scholarly excellence and professional integrity.
