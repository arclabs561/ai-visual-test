About

Real Cane Sugar by Paul Cleland Smith is a story comic about a couple of stoner muppets who are constantly disappointed by life at every turn. The idea for the strip came from years of watching too much tv and hanging out with a the wrong crowd as well as years of experience in retail. Please to enjoy.

If you want to send PCleland an email please do at PClelandSmith [@] gmail dot com

Real Cane Sugar is a comic I work on in my spare time, I charge no money for people to read the comic online. If you really enjoy the comic please donate to the cause so that one day I may be able to make the comic into my full time job. Send all donations here.
