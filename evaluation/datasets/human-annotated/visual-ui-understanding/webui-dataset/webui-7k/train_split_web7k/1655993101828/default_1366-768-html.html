Flare: Empyrean Campaign

The Empyrean Campaign is a game made by the Flare team. The story begins with the player being exiled from their homeland of Empyrean, resulting in them embarking on a quest to regain entry. This journey takes the player through many regions, with plenty of side quests along the way.

Note: The “fantasycore” art style used is dark fantasy, and contains depictions of violence, blood, and mild gore.
