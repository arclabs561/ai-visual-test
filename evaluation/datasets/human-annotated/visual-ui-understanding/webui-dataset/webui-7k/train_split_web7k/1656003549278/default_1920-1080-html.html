It's a requirement

Most countries have privacy laws that require you to include a privacy policy. iubenda makes it easy to comply with legal requirements across multiple countries' legislations (including the GDPR).

Policies are invalid if they're missing the right information

In order to be compliant, your policy must describe the personal data collected and the purposes of their collection and list all the third parties the data is shared with.

"Is the US covered?"

The US is one of the countries our legal team monitors most closely. Our legal text is modeled after the strictest privacy requirements found in the world like the US's CCPA & CalOPPA, the GDPR and ePrivacy. We also regularly monitor all major global legislations to ensure that our solutions continue to meet necessary requirements.

For the CCPA

More on 360° CCPA Compliance

The often compared to the EU's GDPR, the Californian Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants additional rights to Californian consumers and places several new requirements on businesses, including disclosure and opt-out requirements. Our solution makes it easy for you to create CCPA Compliant Privacy Policies by:

  • Displaying CCPA related language, disclosures, and instructions as legally required
  • Indicating services active on your site which might constitute a sale under the CCPA definition (as legally required)
  • Automatically updating your embedded privacy policy with the CCPA text once activated within the generator – no need to re-integrate the code on your site

FAQ

Should my email newsletters contain a privacy policy?

Yes. Email newsletters should contain a reference to your privacy policy. You must also show a link to your privacy policy when users subscribe to your newsletter in order to inform the user about your data use/collection practices.

More on newsletter legal requirements

Can I just copy and paste the generated legal text?

From time to time laws and third-party requirements are amended and updated; it's important to ensure that your policies meet these latest requirements. For this reason, we use embedding and NOT copy & paste. With this method, you can rest assured that your policy is up to date and being maintained remotely by our legal team.
