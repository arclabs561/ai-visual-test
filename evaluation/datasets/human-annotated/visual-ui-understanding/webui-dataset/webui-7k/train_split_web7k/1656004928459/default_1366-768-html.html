We are Floryn

We're one of the fastest growing Fintechs in The Netherlands and soon maybe in Europe as well. With our innovative technology we're uprooting the financial sector and helping many companies — small and large — with fast, simple and flexible business loans with an amazing customer experience and service.

Floryn has originated over €200 million in business loans, all within our first 5 years. We're making an impact: we are helping companies grow, create jobs, and realise ambitions. The next milestone? €1 billion! We're not a bank but entrepreneurs — just like our customers. We're passionate about fixing business loans and take pride in our work.

We are looking for the very best talent there is, ranging from full-stack engineers and data scientists to growth hackers and sales managers. Quality comes first with everything we do, and it shows. We also like to have fun, both at work and outside the office, with drinks and many team activities. Our team of over 50 persons consists of young but experienced and well-educated people with plenty of energy!

Building great stuff together is what drives us. We develop all our technology in-house, with modern open-source technologies. Quality always comes first.

We've built our own credit models powered by machine learning combined with humans. We help our customers with an in-house online lending platform and payment systems. We work with thousands of customers and millions of data points every day. We are licensed by the Dutch Central Bank under PSD2 in The Netherlands

How we work

Develop yourself

We have a yearly education budget, that can be expanded if need arises, because we want to make sure that you can develop yourself. We're also a fast growing company, where new opportunities present themselves often.

Be comfortable, have fun

We make sure you can work well with a Macbook Pro and all the high-end gear you need. With paid time off, a good salary and a pension fund. We also have many regular regular events varying from drinks to sporting activities.

Remote or at the office

We have a fantastic office in 's-Hertogenbosch, but most of our people work at least partly remote. Working hours are flexible, although we do want to create some overlap. We have excellent 1st class travel compensation.
