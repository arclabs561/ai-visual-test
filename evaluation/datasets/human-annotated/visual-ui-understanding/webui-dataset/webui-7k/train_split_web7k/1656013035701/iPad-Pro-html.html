SUPERIOR HARDWARE & NETWORKS

Our Shared and Resellers plans are deployed on high performance pure SSD drives and served from robust hardware. Our minimum server specifications, at current are:

  • 2x Intel Xeon Octo-Core E5 2660
  • 64 GB DDR3 Memory
  • 3.2TB of NVME based Storage
  • 1Gbps Network Uplink Port

BuyShared offers the same datacenter locations, and the same quality networks as we do with BuyVM:

Las Vegas, US (FiberHub): Our Las Vegas datacenter offers a safe and secure environment for shared web hosting - top of the line 24/7 security with dual authentication access control, video and security monitoring. FiberHub is also equipped with Cummins diesel generators, N+1 redundant cooling systems, multiple diverse fiber providers in the facility (building is located just 200 yards from a major fiber route), and multiple provider POP’s in the facility.

Roost, LU (root S.A.): Roost, Luxembourg was selected for protecting customer privacy. It is an excellent location for freedom of speech content and content that would receive great scrutiny in places like the United States. Freedom of speech is protected in Luxembourg with minimal limitations.

Luxembourg has consistently ranked near the top of the World Press Freedom Index (Top 10 percentile) every year since 2008. Customer protection, privacy, data storage, collection and handling are well defined and protected under multiple laws in Luxembourg.

Currently, there is no DMCA takedown enforcement in Luxembourg.

Roost, Luxembourg is central to all major European markets. It's a fully built Tier 4 data center with all the features you expect. Roost has a peered international network with over 1000 BGP sessions to over 500 different networks. There is direct peering in Roost to the largest internet exchanges in Europe: LINX, DE-CIX, AMS-IX, FranceIX and Equinix.
