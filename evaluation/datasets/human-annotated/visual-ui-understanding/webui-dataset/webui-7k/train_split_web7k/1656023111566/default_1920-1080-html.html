RECOIL

A longform webcomic.

Recoil is a comic that I update weekly at recoil.one. I have been telling this story since 2014.

The comic is sketched and inked traditionally on bristol board, then scanned in to be colored and lettered digitally. Readers get to watch a page develop each week by pledging to my patreon.
