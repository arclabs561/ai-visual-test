Contact Us

Before contacting us, please review the Frequently Asked Questions, Student Guide and other Resources that will answer most questions.

  • Have questions or want to discuss Google Summer of Code? You can ask on the discussion list. You can also join the #gsoc irc channel and chat with other participants and mentors. Please don't spam the discussion list with personal details about your specific situation as there are 15K people on the list.

  • Questions for a Google Program Administrator (for example: questions about your specific situation or about the website?) Email gsoc-support@google.com.
