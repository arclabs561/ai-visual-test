The Montauk Beach House Hotel Review

The 5-star Montauk Beach House Hotel is placed in Montauk, not far from Kirk Park Beach. This venue has an outdoor pool as well as free self-parking, an outdoor swimming pool and a sun terrace.

Pros

  • Main touristic hotel in an absolutely perfect setting.
  • Terrific service provided.

Cons

  • A few past guests were dissatisfied with too soft beds.
  • Minor complaints about a few kind of rude employees.
