Into the Ruins

Into the Ruins is a quarterly deindustrial science fiction magazine published by Figuration Press. The magazine focuses on publishing speculative fiction that explores a future defined by natural limits, energy and resource depletion, industrial decline, climate change, and other consequences stemming from the reckless and shortsighted exploitation of our planet, and to imagine the ways that humans will adapt, survive, live, die, and thrive within this future.

Into the Ruins recently finished its second year of publication and will soon begin Year Three. Eight issues have been published as of March 2018, and Year One and Year Two packages are currently available.

Interested in the magazine? You can subscribe and purchase individual issues on the Into the Ruins website. You can read about the magazine’s philosophy or catch up with its blog. Or you can click on any of the issue covers below to go to the individual issue pages on the Into the Ruins website–or use the links below the covers to purchase issues directly from Figuration Press or buy from Amazon. (Please note the magazines are available through traditional distribution channels, as well, so feel free to ask your local independent bookseller to order copies for you!)

Or jump right in (or introduce a friend or family member) with Year One (Issues #1-4) or Year Two (Issues #5-8) packages for just $43 each, shipped FREE in the U.S.
