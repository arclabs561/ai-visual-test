Syfon Studio

We deal in visual interaction. Years of experience and solid research allow us to create cutting edge visual concepts. We believe that communication is a subtle and precise instrument that needs to be operated with knowledge and care. That is why we put effort in analyzing ongoing transformation processes and new media development. We still consider design as an adventure.

be in t f

Lab

The desire for staying up-to-date, expanding our toolbox and redesigning creative process encouraged us to launch some self-initiated, cooperative or experimental projects. We treat them as a training ground, a space for uninhibited idea exchange and a source of great fun and satisfaction. The laboratory is open to failures and arguments – goals are elusive and the paths are winding.
