Warden FAQ
What is a veBoost and who uses them?
A veBoost is the delegated version of a boost in the CRV ecosystem. User's depositing in eligible Curve gauges can increase their CRV rewards by boosting their multiplier (up to 2.5x). VeCRV holders who don't need the boost can sell their boosts via Warden to Curve LPs who want to maximize their yields.
What happens to seller's tokens?
Your funds never leave your wallet. Warden creates veBoosts from a user's veCRV balance then the veBoost is applied to the buyer's address. Seller's keep full control and can continue to use their veCRV for Curve Gauges, Gauge Weights votes, and DAO votes.
If I use Warden will I lose my bribes?
No, they're related to different utilities of veCRV. You can sell boosts and claim gauge weight bribes separately.
What are the fees?
The platform takes 5% on buys. All fees are in CRV.
Can I sell and use a boost at the same time?
Yes. Through the seller settings you can limit how much of your balance is put on the market.
Is Warden audited?
There's no planned audit. Since funds are never staked in Warden there's minimal smart contract risk. However, use the app at your own discretion.
