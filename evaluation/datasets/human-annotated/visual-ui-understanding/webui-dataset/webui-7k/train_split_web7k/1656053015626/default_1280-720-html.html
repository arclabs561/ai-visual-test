software for musicians

My name is Vera Kinter aka Artvera. Welcome to my site!

My interests include computer music and graphic art. I have been creating sound patches and graphics for many great VST instruments, for example Protoplasm, Wusikstation, Abakos Pro, Phrazor, Stealth and many more.

I cooperated with the best developers and with companies like Camel Audio, Wusik.com, Back In Time Records, H.G.Fortune, Pro-Sounds, Sonicbytes, Pluggotic, Nucleus SoundLabs, Dangerous Bear, Herc's Music Systems and others.

Below you can see a list of resources available here. I hope that you will enjoy them and find them to be useful.
