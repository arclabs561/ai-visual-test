How we roll!

Guiding principles of our team

Ethics First

Our team spends a lot of time and energy on putting ethics before financial gains. It is not easy, but also a principle we don’t negotiate.

Free Open Source

Free open source software gives you more freedoms over your infrastructure. That’s why all the instances we offer are open source.

Tech Freedom

At Cloud68.co we work hard on making sure YOU are in control of YOUR data. More power to you, more freedom from the big tech.

Privacy Sensitive

We take your privacy seriously and we will use our knowledge to do whatever is possible to keep your data private. We promise to stand up to this!

Decentralized ftw

For almost a decade members of our collective have been heavily involved with internet freedom related projects. We don’t plan to slow down!

Indie Web

Our vision for a greater indie web aligns with some more principles drafted from people we really respect:

indieweb.org/principles
