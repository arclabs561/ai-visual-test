Recruit Talent

Reaching the top takes the top talent. ARIZONA@WORK helps employers throughout the state to meet their recruitment and training needs by offering the following no-cost services.

  • Post jobs

    Post your job vacancies on the state’s largest jobs database, Arizona Job Connection. Unlike most online job posting sites, creating an employer account and posting job openings on the Arizona Job Connection is absolutely FREE.
    Post jobs

  • Search Resumes

    Search thousands of resumes listed on Arizona Job Connection and let our system’s innovative technology match eligible candidates to your vacancies at no-cost to you.
    Search resumes

  • Unemployment Insurance Taxes

    Our team can help you simplify your Unemployment Insurance (UI) Taxes. We offer electronic reporting options to expedite the process and have a team of UI Tax specialists to clarify all you need to know.
    Access UI tax information

  • Training Programs

    Gain access to customized training for your employees who require additional workforce skills through the Arizona Job Training Program.
    Search training programs for your business

  • Recruiting Resources

    ARIZONA@WORK can provide your company with interview space and other customized services to help you hire many employees quickly.
    Search recruiting resources for your business

  • Offer Apprenticeships

    A Registered Apprenticeship develops a strong workforce that delivers a powerful economic boost to your company. Quickly get employees up to speed to maximize the abilities of your workforce.
    Learn more about apprenticeships

  • Research Labor Market and Wages

    ARIZONA@WORK can help make sense of the changing labor landscape with data that bring the economic and occupational picture of your market area into focus.
    Learn Arizona’s current labor market information

  • Workforce Reduction Support

    For businesses that anticipate downsizing or restructuring, ARIZONA@WORK Rapid Response Services can help meet the needs of companies and employees with layoff aversion, pre-layoff assistance, and other specialized support.
    Find worker reduction support

  • Employer Success Stories

    We take the great pride in the people we’ve assisted, from dedicated employers creating job opportunities to motivated individuals putting their talents to work. We are very pleased to share some of their stories.
    Read employer success stories

  • Federal Bonding Program

    The Federal Bonding Program is a hiring incentive that provides employers a minimum of $5,000 in fidelity bond insurance for a six-month period at no cost and no deductible for at-risk job seekers.
    Learn more
