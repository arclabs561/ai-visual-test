Evil in Technicolor: a New Horror Anthology for Release in October 2020!

By editorialmonster on June 1, 2020 • ( 2 )

VernacularBooks is thrilled to announce the forthcoming horror and dark fantasy anthology EVIL IN TECHNICOLOR edited by Joe M McDermott, and featuring the work of 10 authors: Stina Leicht, E. Catherine Tobler, A. C. Wise, Rhiannon Rasmussen-Silverstein, Adam Gallardo, Haralambi Markov, Craig Laurance Gidney, Bonnie Jo Stufflebeam, Molly Tanzer, and Nick Mamatas. This anthology features ten novelettes from ten different, and amazing talents.

The stories run the gamut from Hammer Horror-inspired literary monster mashes to classic haunted houses full of old secrets, and to supernatural and uncanny happenings of all sorts. The stories initially were written to be inspired by old Hammer Horror films, and films of their black and white and technicolor contemporaries. Some are more psychological and strange than terrifying, while others are soaked in blood and fear. These artful, intelligent, and thrilling tales of terror will be available wherever fine books are sold on October 1, 2020.
