Simple Emoji Licensing

Free

  • Download all 3,342 emoji icons for free!
  • Free for any personal use, including students and teachers
  • Business may use for testing purposes only without premium license
  • PNG files up to 128px

Premium

  • Online royalty-free, attribution-free, perpetual digital license
  • SVG files (vector) and PNG (512px)
  • Available for all content including emoji, stickers, and animations
  • Prices vary based on organization size (up to 249 employees)

Enterprise

  • Custom license with warranties & protections
  • Required for agencies or companies with 250+ employees
  • Required for large budget campaigns & productions
  • Price varies, fast high level service and support
