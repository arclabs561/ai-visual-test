How is this different from Nansen, Moby or other apps?
Ninjalerts is singularly focused on making the best, easiest to use, and most convenient NFT alerts product on the market. Many NFT tools try to do everything and what you end up with is a tool that does nothing well. Our focus is not to tell you what's going on, but to tell you when to act now. And we will be 10x better than any other tool at doing this because of our focus. Currently, we are the only tool on the market with a web app and mobile app that syncs so you can take your alerts on the go. We're also the only tool delivering notifications through push and browser. Other tools like Nansen and Moby only offer discord, telegram, or SMS, which are either inconvenient or unreliable. Moby only has wallet watching (not contracts), and doesn't allow you to customize more than just seeing mints, buys, and sells. Not only can we show you alerts for contracts and when wallets mint, buy, or sell, on your phone and through push/browser alerts, but we plan to add more filtering so you can set additional conditions such as "buys for over 5 ETH" or "mints at least 3" OR "alert me when 2 people in this list of 10 both mint something" etc. There are hundreds of additional filters that can be added, and a team that's not focused on this full-time won't be able to implement these essential but advanced features. Furthermore, we run our own nodes on the blockchain and use the blockchain as the single source of truth. Our notifications are faster than Metamask and Etherscan! We believe we have the best and simplest user experience, and that we have a very fair price.
