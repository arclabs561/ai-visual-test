Council & Staff

Elected Councillors serve based on the fiscal year (December 1 to November 30).  Officers and the Councillor at Large are elected for one year.  Regional Councillors are elected for two-year terms, staggered East and West.  Public Member appointments are made by the Lieutenant Governor and are for three-year terms.
