Basis functions in OpenSlides

Agenda

Complement, change or move automatically numbered agenda items via drag & drop in the running system. Connect single items with lists of speakers and speaking times and show inofficial items like breaks for better scheduling.

Learn more

Motions

Capture motions with applicant and text. Assign categories and motion numbers. Log the resolution and polling results during the motion discussion. The flexible filtering helps you while searching in all motions and their changes.

Learn more

Elections

Propose candidates off the list of participants. Generate ad-hoc personalized ballots for secret elections. Log and project the counted votes – for all participants accessible in OpenSlides.

Learn more

Projectors

OpenSlides is designed for working with multiple screens and comes with a powerful projector management. The web interface allows the projection and editing of slides while the projectors are running in full screen.

Learn more

Electronic voting

Use live voting for motions and elections. The participant group that is eligible to vote can be defined per motion/election. Only participants set to present and selected as eligible to vote in OpenSlides are allowed to vote. Nominal or non-nominal (anonymous) voting on motions and elections are possible. Individual vote weighting and vote transfers are also possible.

Learn more

Video livestream

Integrate a video livestream of your virtual assembly in OpenSlides. All participants can follow the always-visible stream in OpenSlides. If a person wants to speak, he/she can put him/herself on the current list of speakers and thereby automatically switches to a Jitsi video conference room. The livestream broadcasts the video conference. After the speaker is finished, the conference room is left and one can follow the livestream again.

Learn more
