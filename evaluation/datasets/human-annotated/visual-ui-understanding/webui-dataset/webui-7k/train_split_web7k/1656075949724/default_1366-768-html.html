Our Mission

Empire Mock Trial is an education nonprofit that offers academic extracurricular programs for high school students.

Each program features four mock trials, during which students argue a case that is legally complex and socially relevant. Outside of the courtroom, students connect with peers from across the globe, volunteering at a local service project and sightseeing in a vibrant city.

Through our programs, we strive to educate, connect and empower young people to be more compassionate and capable citizens.
