Procedures

Our web development process places a premium on keeping you directly engaged not only with your project manager, but especially with the designer who is carrying out your vision. We also adapt our processes based on the size of the project. Some projects require detailed documentation, schedules, and progress reporting while simpler projects do not. Our client’s sites have been successful because of the personal attention we provide to meet their wants and needs.

 

 

Search Engine Visibility

All websites we deliver are developed specifically to be ‘search engine ready’ or ‘search engine friendly’. Meta tags are added to support keywords, description, title, and search engine directions. Alt tags are added to images that match the client’s content to improve search engine results. Extra efforts to improve search engine rankings require additional investment. 

 

 

Browser Support

Current standard web browsers include the latest two versions of Internet Explorer and the most recent update of Mozilla Firefox. All our sites are tested in each of these environments. If a specific browser not listed is required please let us know. Knowing this information up front will help us when deciding which technologies to employ for developing the site based on the limitations of the specific browser requested.
