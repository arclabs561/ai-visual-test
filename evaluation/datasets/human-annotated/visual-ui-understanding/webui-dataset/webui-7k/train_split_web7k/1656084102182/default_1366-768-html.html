An energetic dance band from Boston, San Francisco, and Vermont, the Free Raisins fuse New England roots with a modern groove. They promise a versatile mix of supremely danceable tunes—come out and get your two scoops worth!
The Free Raisins are:
Audrey Knuth—Fiddle
Jeff Kaufman—Mandolin, Brass, Feet
Amy Englesberg—Piano, Accordion
