Focus Areas Our range of work is guided by the outcomes we seek, with many of our efforts overlapping and converging across multiple focus areas.

  • Courageous Leadership

    The challenges of today and tomorrow require discerning leaders who approach solutions with innovative mindsets, unafraid of challenging the status quo.

    Examples

    The Collaborative Leaders Network

    The Collaborative Leaders Network
    is committed to solving critical issues in the state of Hawaii through collaboration and open dialogue. Recent efforts focused on the development of the State’s early childhood program, mediation for a decade-old water rights dispute on Maui, and support for the Freshwater Initiative, a project of the Hawaii Community Foundation that seeks to find ways to increase Hawaii’s water supplies. The organization is also dedicated to cultivating a community of trained facilitators in the islands.

    The Omidyar Fellows

    As the cornerstone initiative of the Hawaii Leadership Forum, The Omidyar Fellows program, helps strengthen the future of Hawaii by equipping diverse groups of emerging leaders with the skills and relationships needed to collectively transform the state. Fellows begin with an intensive 15-month curriculum designed to build stronger leaders and cross-sector connections and continue their lifelong commitment to affect societal change through the Forum of Fellows.

    The Omidyar Fellowship at Santa Fe Institute

    The Omidyar Fellowship at Santa Fe Institute aims to create “new leadership for new science.” This fellowship offers early-career scholars a residence of up to three years in a collaborative research community that provides cross-disciplinary opportunities to study complex problems that advance scientific frontiers.

  • Good Governance

    Wise decisions emerge from effective governance that is just, responsive, and sensitive to our interdependencies. We believe a strong democracy respects individual liberty and privacy, values citizen engagement, and requires government transparency and accountability.

    Examples

    Civil Beat Law Center for the Public Interest

    The Civil Beat Law Center for the Public Interest works to develop solutions that promote an open, transparent and responsive government. The Center has led a number of efforts in support of its mission – including lobbying the Legislature to update the open meetings law for the Internet age and provide greater access to information before government meetings and organizing local media and nonprofits to encourage the Governor to embrace more progressive policies toward public records access. The Center has also challenged the Honolulu Police Department’s policy to deny all public records requests for files concerning police officers suspended for misconduct.

    The Democracy Fund

    The Democracy Fund’s Governance program is focused on principled leadership and an effective Congress. By fostering better, more trusting relationships among political leaders, encouraging greater civility, and increasing their capacity to work together to solve problems, the Governance program aims to help our elected leaders deliberate, negotiate, and serve the American people.

    Additionally, the Democracy Fund’s Elections program is focused on modern elections and the role of money in politics. By funding unbiased research, highlighting ways to improve elections administration, and encouraging bipartisan solutions as well as breakthrough ideas, the Elections program aims to ensure that the views and votes of the public come first in our democracy.

    Luminate

    By supporting courageous, innovative organisations and entrepreneurs around the world, Luminate fights for greater public participation, institutional transparency and accountability, individuals’ digital and data rights, and press freedom and independence.

    Nicolas Berggruen Institute

    Pierre Omidyar serves as a member of the Nicolas Berggruen Institute’s 21st Century committee, a forum for dialogue on global governance.

    Omidyar Network

    Through both for-profit investments and nonprofit grants, Omidyar Network’s Governance & Citizen Engagement initiative invests in organizations that support greater empowerment for citizens and more effective, efficient, and fair government and allocation of resources.

    Omidyar Network India

    Omidyar Network India seeks to strengthen the relationship between citizens and government. It invests in entrepreneurs with innovative web and mobile tools that make citizen participation easier and more meaningful, and help governments respond to citizens’ needs. Omidyar Network India also supports civil society organizations and innovative start-ups that define best practices around governments’ disclosure of information.

  • Informed & Engaged Citizens

    A healthy society relies on citizens being engaged and informed. We support a free and robust press and quality journalism to give citizens the accurate information they need to hold those in power accountable.

    Examples

    The Democracy Fund

    The Democracy Fund’s Public Square program invests in innovations and institutions that are reinventing local media and expanding the “public square” to ensure that people can access diverse sources of information and different points of view. The Public Square program supports efforts to combat misinformation deepen individuals’ engagement in civic life though new venues for reasoned debate and deliberation.

    First Look Media

    First Look Media – a bold independent media company that empowers the most ambitious voices in journalism, arts and entertainment. Launched by eBay founder and philanthropist, Pierre Omidyar, First Look Media today operates across several areas, including an entertainment studio, Topic, which develops, produces and finances feature films, documentaries, television and digital content; the newly launched digital storytelling destination, Topic.com; the award-winning investigative journalism outlet, The Intercept; the critically acclaimed documentary film unit, Field of Vision; and the popular political satire cartoon, The Nib. The company’s first feature film, “Spotlight,” won the 2016 Academy Award® for Best Picture.

    Honolulu Civil Beat

    Honolulu Civil Beat is an award-winning investigative and watchdog online media enterprise aimed at informing and engaging community members through public affairs and investigative reporting on topics of critical importance to Hawaii.

    Humanity United

    Humanity United engages and supports media partners, reporters and storytellers to raise awareness and educate key audiences about important social issues. Humanity United supports The Guardian’s media platform titled “Modern Day Slavery in Focus,” a series investigating human trafficking and exploitation around the world.

    Luminate

    Luminate supports organizations that are committed to defending a vibrant, free press that uncovers the truth and holds power to account. It also works to enable people to shape the decisions that affect their lives and access the services they need, with a focus on those groups that are marginalised or underserved.

    Omidyar Network India

    Omidyar Network India supports independent journalism that reports on issues concerning citizens and civil society through support such as equity investments in new business models.

    The World Post

    An advocate for quality journalism, Pierre Omidyar serves on the editorial board of the World Post, a platform for understanding current events through a global lens.

  • Thriving Communities

    We are all connected through a global community, and when people and communities are empowered and respectful of their interdependencies, societies flourish.

    Examples

    Hopelab

    Hopelab is a social innovation lab focused on designing science-based technologies to improve the health and well-being of teens and young adults. Projects include a collaboration with Nurse-Family Partnership, where we are working to help scale the program by better meeting the needs of today’s “digital native” teen mothers; improving the treatment and survivorship outcomes for adolescents and young adults with cancer; and improving the availability of tech-enabled mental and behavioral health interventions for adolescents.

    Humanity United

    With a belief that human freedom is paramount to an individual’s ability to thrive, Humanity United works to abolish practices of modern day slavery across the globe by engaging partners from nonprofits, media, government, and business to be part of the solution. Recognizing that the private sector can be a force for good, Humanity United collaborated with industry thought leaders to launch KnowTheChain, a resource that people business and investors understand and address forced labor abuses within their supply chain.

    Humanity United believes that all people deserve to live in a peaceful world where they have the opportunity to seek their full potential. Unfortunately, fundamental peace and security are increasingly at risk and international order is being challenged in ways we haven’t seen before. Humanity United’s Transforming Peacebuilding strategy seeks to support diverse and dynamic national and local partners in Zimbabwe and Mali to develop and advance their own approaches to building the conditions necessary for peace.

    Imaginable Futures

    Imaginable Futures (IF) is a global philanthropic investment firm that believes learning has the power to unlock human potential and aspires to provide every learner with the opportunity and the tools they need to imagine, and to realize, a brighter future. By taking a systems approach to solving complex education challenges, IF works across public, private and social sectors to bring to life transformational ideas in local, national and global contexts for learners of all ages. With a deep commitment to on-the-ground partnership and co-creation with those we serve, Imaginable Futures is empowering learners, families and communities to be the changemakers of the future. Established in 2020 and managed by a global team, Imaginable Futures has invested $200 million in more than 100 partners across Africa, Latin America and the United States, as well as in India with sister organization, Omidyar Network India.

    Omidyar Network

    Omidyar Network seeks to create a more equitable economy, promote responsible technology that improves lives, expand human capability and discover the emergent issues that will shape the future.

    Omidyar Network India

    Omidyar Network India makes equity investments in early stage enterprises and provide grants to non-profits in the areas of Digital Identity, Education, Emerging Tech, Financial Inclusion, Governance and Citizen Engagement, and Property Rights.

    Omidyar-Tufts Microfinance Fund

    Pierre and Pam Omidyar partnered with their alma matter, Tufts University, to create a $100 million Omidyar-Tufts Microfinance Fund. The fund bolsters international microfinance institutions in their efforts to scale capacity to make loans to poor to launch their own entrepreneurial pursuits.

    Ulupono Initiative

    Ulupono Initiative aims to improve the quality of life for island residents in part by increasing the use of clean, renewable energy and the production of locally grown food. Ulupono’s energy investments each contribute to decreasing Hawaii’s dependency on imported fossil fuels, including Honolulu Seawater Air Conditioning, which is developing one of Hawaii’s largest energy efficiency projects with a district cooling system in downtown Honolulu, and Bikeshare Hawaii, which recently introduced Biki, Honolulu’s new bikeshare program. In the food sector, Ulupono has funded local, pasture-raised beef projects, such as Paniolo Cattle Company, and the Hawaii Farm-to-School Initiative, which aims to include more local food on public school menus.
