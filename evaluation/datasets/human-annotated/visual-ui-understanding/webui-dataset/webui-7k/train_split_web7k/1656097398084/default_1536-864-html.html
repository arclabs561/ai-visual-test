Estate Administration

One of our estate administration attorneys can help you make the best decisions for your family’s financial future. The Stanley Law Group offers legal services in:

  • Will drafting
  • Creating trusts
  • Probating an estate
  • Creating power of attorney documents

A power of attorney is a legal document that grants a chosen person, or a representative, the legal right to act on a person’s behalf. A healthcare power of attorney gives an individual the authority to act on someone’s behalf when medical professionals deem them unable to make their own decisions. Our estate attorneys have years of experience drafting these important legal documents for our Columbia clients.

In terms of estate administration, a wills and trusts lawyer from the Stanley Law Group can help you to decide which of the two would work best for your financial situation. Don’t leave your family to make difficult decisions on your behalf in the event of your passing, Take the time needed to meet with one of our probate lawyers to prepare ahead of time.
