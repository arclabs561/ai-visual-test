Ottawa Backpackers Inn offers the cheapest tourist accommodation in Ottawa. We also have no hidden membership fees. We’re an ideally located hostel in downtown Ottawa – we’re close to almost everything you will want to see and do (activities, restaurants and bars). We’re also conveniently close to a supermarket and liquor store.

How do you get here, you may ask? Well whether you’re arriving by car, plane, train, or Greyhound bus, we have a set of handy instructions for you to find your way to the hostel from the station.

Explore our website to find out about all the fun activities we offer and the cool things that you can do in Ottawa. When you’re having a look around you’ll also see info on our excellent rates.
