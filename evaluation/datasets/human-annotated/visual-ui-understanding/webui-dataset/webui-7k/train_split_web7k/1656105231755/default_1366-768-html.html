Non-profits

We work with charity and non-profit organisations to provide better services to their clients through bespoke fundraising portals, dedicated management systems and more.

Our Software Development Solutions for Non-profits

Our expertise can help you focus on the problems you need to solve in your not-for-profit organisation – and the technology that can help you do just that.

Whether you need to raise funds faster, manage campaigns more efficiently, sell products consistently or present content to your audience in the most compelling way possible, our software solutions can help your not-for-profit organisation maximise productivity and reach more donors than ever before.

Some of our specific non-profit solutions have included:

  • Transforming the royalties claim process for the Design & Artists Copyright Society with an intuitive portal and payments reconciliation and distribution system, resulting in the volume of claims in 2020 increasing by several thousand more than in previous years.
  • Building a grant management system to help London-based charity Water Aid track funds and manage their spending. The end result offered the company greater insight for far less time invested per user and allowed for further projects with DCSL GuideSmiths exploring integration with other systems.
  • Designing and launching a matchmaking portal empowered The Good Exchange to fulfil its remit of helping funders find just causes to donate to. Following that success, The Good Exchange then partnered with us again to explore other opportunities, including transforming donor journeys in their new matchmaking platform.

View more of our non-profit case studies

Digital Non-Profit Solutions You Can Trust

As fully accredited Microsoft Gold Application Development Partners, Cloud Solution Providers, and integration specialists, DCSL GuideSmiths solutions can be trusted to work intuitively alongside any systems your charity or non-profit organisation uses.

We are also a Microsoft Silver Partner for Data Analytics. That means we’re trusted by charities and other organisations for projects involving connecting data sources, performing data transformations, and modelling and visualising data.

DCSL GuideSmiths were one of the world’s first companies to achieve the ISO 9001 global standard for quality. That means every non-profit digital solution we design can be trusted to meet the highest possible standards.

Plus, our Cyber Essentials Plus certification means you can trust us to safeguard your not-for-profit organisation from cyber threats.
