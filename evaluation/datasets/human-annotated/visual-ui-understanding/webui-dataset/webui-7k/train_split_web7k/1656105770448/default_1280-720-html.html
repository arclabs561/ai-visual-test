April Update

by Sarah Posted on April 25, 2022

Since my last post I’ve been get­ting a nov­el ready for query­ing, tak­ing on more duties at Forge Lit­er­ary Mag­a­zine as co-man­ag­ing edi­tor, and man­ag­ing some epilep­sy issues. All of that has forced me to take a step back from post­ing book rec­om­men­da­tions here for a while, but I’m hop­ing to even­tu­al­ly return. 

In the mean­time, check out all the bril­liant work we’re pub­lish­ing over at Forge this spring! As for me, I have a short sto­ry forth­com­ing in Hyper­text, which I will link when it goes live. Tip your wait­staff and enjoy this pho­to of a ver­nal pool in the woods behind my house, full of tad­poles and potential.
