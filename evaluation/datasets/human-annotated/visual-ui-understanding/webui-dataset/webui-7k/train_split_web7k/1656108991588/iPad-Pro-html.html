Write Away™ Writing Workshops

Lana facilitates writing workshops that create a supportive, safe, and nurturing environment where writers can express their unique voices.

She provides in-class writing activities to stimulate the senses, challenge, and inspire.

After each writing session, participants share their work and provide each other with only POSITIVE feedback, expressing what they liked, what touched them, what techniques were effective.

By providing feedback that focuses on the strengths of the writing, writers come to understand the value of their own work and trust their voices. Participants learn from the strengths they see in each others' work new techinques that they might want to adopt for themselves.

Workshops can vary from day-long to weekend getaways to multi-week sessions.
