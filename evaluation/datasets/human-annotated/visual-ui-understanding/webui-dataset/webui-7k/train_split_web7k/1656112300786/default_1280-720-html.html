Boolean
Planet

28.07.17
Work

Boolean Planet is an interactive installation created by FutureWife with music by Max Cooper. The audience can play with a giant inflatable sphere to morph generative sound and visuals, programmed using multi-channel spatialization.

The narrative visual thread is to tell the story of how stars and planets can form from the quark soup of the big bang using abstract planetary landscapes and organic animations.

The project also involves Vincent Houzé - director of Max Cooper's video for 'Seed' - and Beau Burrows of FutureWife.

Boolean Planet will be exhibited at Panorama festival in New York from 28-30 July 2017.
