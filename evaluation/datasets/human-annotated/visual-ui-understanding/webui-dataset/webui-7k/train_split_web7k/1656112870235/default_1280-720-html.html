Clogged Drains

​If you are reading this part of our website, one can only assume you have a clogged drain in your home or business. We can also assume that you have unsuccessfully tried to unclog the problem with a plunger or drain cleaner. At Sterling Heights Plumbing, we are specialists at unclogging drains. We have the most technologically advanced equipment to unclog your drain without damaging any of your pipes. Pouring certain chemicals down your drain or improper plunging has the potential to damage your pipes so leave the clogged drains to the expert plumbers. If you have a clogged drain, Sterling Heights Plumbing will dispatch a plumber to your location quickly to get the problem resolved. Because we have a fairly large staff of experts, we typically have a plumber available to come out and fix your clogged drain. There are many things that can cause a clogged drain that include: dirt, skin, hair, soap scum, paper products, and other foreign objects. Whatever the case, your friends at Sterling Heights Plumbing will fix the problem quickly and economically. 

Pipe Leaks / Cracked Pipes

​​If you have a pipe leak or a cracked pipe in your home or business, you should call a plumber right away. The longer you wait to get these things fixed, the more water damage will occur. If you have a pipe leak or cracked pipe that is currently causing water damage, the first thing you should do is try to shut the water off. Typically, most toilets, sinks, and water end sources have some type of shut off valve located nearby. If you are unable to find a local shut off valve, you may need to shut off the entire water to the home or businesses. This water shut off valve is usually located in a basement or utility room. Once you have stopped the water, you should call an experienced plumber at Sterling Heights Plumbing to repair the problem. Any plumber at our office has the knowledge to either repair or replace the pipe quickly and economically and get your water service operational. If you are experiencing a pipe leak or cracked pipe, give us a call right now and we will dispatch a plumber. Many of the calls we receive in the wintertime are because of frozen pipes that crack or burst and can cause major water damage if not discovered quickly. If you have a cracked pipe due to your pipes freezing, give us a call and we will be out quickly to fix it.
