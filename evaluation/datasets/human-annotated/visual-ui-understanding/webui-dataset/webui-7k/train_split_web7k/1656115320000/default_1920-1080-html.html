Helping public bodies to publish high-quality, reusable Open Data.

The Derilinx team has been leading Linked and Open Data developments, both in Ireland and internationally, since 2010. We have a proven track record working with both public and private-sector bodies.

Irish National Open Data Portal – Department of Public Expenditure and Reform

Derilinx provides support to the Department of Public Expenditure and Reform on the national Open Data Initiative, including technical support for the national Open Data portal (data.gov.ie), and guidance on the technical framework and implementation of the national Open Data Strategy 2017-2022

Smart Dublin - Open Data Platform & Services for Smart Cities

The dedicated Open Data Platform (data.smartdublin.ie) is a key element underpinning Smart Dublin. Derilinx also provides client-side expert advisory services in the area of Open Data Best Practice, Data Quality, Ecosystem Management and Analytics/Web Application services.

World Bank Energy - Full Open Data lifecycle, from Open Data Publication to Sustainable Impact

EnergyData.info is an open data platform providing access to datasets and data analytics that are relevant to the energy sector. Derilinx have also developed multiple Web Applications that leverage a range of Open Data Sources to address particular business challenges.
