TDARB.ORG
=========

My name is Bradley Taunt and `tdarb.org` is my personal space on the
internet. I'm a user interface and experience designer by trade.

In my free time I dabble in open source projects, hang out with my wife
and 3 children, cheer on the Ottawa Senators and play video games
(mostly RPGs).

This small piece of the internet is where I write about macOS, design,
CSS, web development, digital privacy, Linux, minimalism (if that
wasn't obvious from this text-based blog) and anything else that
tickles my fancy.

You might know of me from my previous articles and demos that are now
archived as static HTML pages. To avoid link-rot those posts exist
under the file directory:


https://tdarb.org/blog
Those interested in preserving the original HTML content for themselves
locally can fork the project directly:


https://git.sr.ht/~tdarb/tdarb.org
Be aware that no further updates or articles will be posted in the
`blog` directory. All new articles will use one of the new RSS feeds
below.

THIS IS A SHINOBI WEBSITE
-------------------------

In case you hadn't noticed, this website is presented in plain text
format. That's because this site is built by a crude and basic shell
script. It's what I refer to as a "shinobi website". You can find out
more and learn how to create your own at the official project site:


https://shinobi.website

ABOUT ME: DIRECTOR'S CUT
------------------------

If you're reading this far down the page, that means you wish to know
more than what I mention above.

I'm a designer/developer hybrid with over 12 years of professional
experience across large enterprise application software, all the way
down to mom-and-pop shops. I have a strong emphasis on building
maintainable CSS, with a passion for accessibility, minimalism and web
performance.

When I'm not pushing pixels or hacking up code, you can find me
experimenting with the latest CSS, splitting batches of firewood,
cheering on the Ottawa Senators, or hanging out with my wife and 3 kids.
