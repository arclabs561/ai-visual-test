► FAN DISCORD

An ongoing queer sci-fi horror comic about physics.

On the Atlantic coast,
there's a town named PORT CITY—

“a place out of time”

While a tourist destination boasting a popular beach and boardwalk,
Port City is also home to ROMAN LABS, an aerospace-turned-tech
company now floundering in the 1980s tech boom.

Rafael and Royal grew up here.
These are the shores where Kas is beached.
Each of them struggling and desperate for change,
they put into motion something to finally shake
the town and their lives from standstill...

Changing history begins with the machine.
