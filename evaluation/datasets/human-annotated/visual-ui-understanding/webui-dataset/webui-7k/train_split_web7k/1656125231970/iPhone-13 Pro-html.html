SatSat

SatSat is a satellite tracking software, for radio amateurs, scientists or hobbyists.

Pass Prediction

SatSat can predict the next passes for the International Space Station and thousands of satellites

Listen to satellites.

SatSat also helps you to listen to the right active frequency to receive satellites data.

Track Satellites

Maps

SatSat embbed its own maps to work completly offline, without any connections.

Tracks an displays satellites path

Touch any satellites on the map to display its path.

Displays future passes can be added to your calendar.
