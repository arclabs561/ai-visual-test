A Design for un-living, work in progress

 
 

Feature 1

After a months of binging on theoretical physics, ideas began to pop into existence. Here I was exploring dimensionality and misalignment with miscommunication between dimensions.

In the making it became and engagement with tension, torsion and balance.

Found board (hand drilled) and steam bent barvecue sticks

New Forms

This came from thoughts on constraint, working with a more rigid form.

Found box. Barbecue sticks

Scaling up & evolving

Now four times the original models although still some way to go. The process of sculpting has become one of grappling with the elements, a struggle.

Found board and steam bent dowel.

 

The aim is to create a sculptural installation in an imagined landscape.
