Helping people get back to work with dignity.
HOW YOU CAN HELP
All of our clients are provided interview & workplace clothing FREE OF CHARGE. We serve nearly 5000 each year. 


START IN YOUR CLOSET
If you’re clearing closets please consider donating them to Clothes the Deal.  We are still accepting donations.
Our policy has always been that the articles should be cleaned, hung and in excellent repair.
We are reiterating this standard practice with our donors. Arrangements may be made for pick-up or drop-off
by emailing the office through the website or calling Steve DeLeon at 909/524-5344.
Now, more than ever, our services will be a vital component in the job search process & helping our neighbors find dignified work. Our client base is expected to be those most affected by the shutdowns and layoffs,
especially women of color and those in the hospitality and food industry.

LET’S COLLABORATE
CTD’s mission is to build community partnerships in order to provide low-income men, women and youth with professional business attire in order to empower them when reaching for employment and self-sufficiency.
This starts by providing clients the opportunity to achieve parity in their efforts.
If you have a collaborative possibility, idea or thought, LET’S TALK!
No one knows what is around the corner,
but we do know we need to be ready to support the newly unemployed when they get back to looking for work.
 
Since 1995 when our founder Ann Gusiff started this effort as a college-class project,
Clothes the Deal has served over 90,000 job-seekers.
Our work is far from over, but we will need your help during this difficult time.
 
Again, please consider donating your gently worn business clothing, making a financial contribution or
sharing any great ideas for a collaborative opportunity. We’re all in this together and together we’ll make a difference.
 
THANK YOU!!
