      We turn Open Source into an experience

      We are the partner near you for innovative Open Source solutions for the implementation of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Management Software for SMEs (ERP Odoo) and IT Infrastructure Management. Further, we are providing our expertise for purchasing and renewing your Subscriptions for various enterprise solutions like Red Hat, Suse or Puppet.

      Our solutions are adapted to the respective local needs and Camptocamp's experts support you on-site!

      Why
      Camptocamp?

      Our highly qualified and passionate employees and our strong commitment to the "ecosystem" of the Open Source software solutions we use enables Camptocamp to develop individual solutions based on the best and most modern Open Source technologies.
