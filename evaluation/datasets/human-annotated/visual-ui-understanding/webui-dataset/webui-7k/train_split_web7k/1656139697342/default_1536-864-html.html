Collectively, we refer to the Websites, Apps, Social Media Pages, and e-mails as the “Services.”

What Personal Information Do We Collect and How Do We Collect It?

From You

The Services collect personal information directly from you, for example, when you participate in surveys or submit reviews, register an account to access the Services, order publications and products, enter contests or sweepstakes, play games, take quizzes, vote in polls, participate in one of our online forums or communities, or redeem points for gift cards and other rewards. This information includes:

  • E-mail address
  • Postal code
  • Other information that you may choose to provide, including if you participate in our survey panel, such as nickname, gender, age, ethnicity, educational level, occupation, income, marital status, political affiliation, religion, hobbies, number and age range of your children, health issues and your household’s health issues – such as medical conditions, medical conditions of those you give care to, the use of visual/hearing aids, allergies, physical symptoms, and use of prescription and over-the-counter medications

We need to collect personal information in order to provide the Services to you. If you do not provide certain personal information, we may not be able to provide the Services you request. If you disclose any personal information relating to other people to us or to our service providers in connection with the Services, you represent that you have the authority to do so and to permit us to use the personal information in accordance with this Privacy Statement.

From Other Sources

We receive your personal information from other sources, for example:

  • Other businesses owned by Dotdash Meredith, when we have the appropriate agreements in place, including Synapse Group, Inc.
  • Market research companies and third-party survey sponsors that have partnered with us for survey panels, when they share your demographic information (but not your survey answers) with us
  • Publicly available databases
  • Joint marketing partners

Information Automatically Collected

  • Browser and device information

    Certain information is collected by most browsers or automatically through your device, such as your Media Access Control (MAC) address, IP address, computer type (Windows or Mac), screen resolution, operating system name and version, device manufacturer and model, language, Internet browser type and version, and the name and version of the Services (such as the App) you are using.

    IP Address. Your IP address is a number that is automatically assigned to your browser by your Internet Service Provider. An IP address may be identified and logged automatically in our server log files whenever a user accesses the Services, along with the time of the visit and the pages visited. We may also derive your approximate location from your IP address.

    Geolocation Data. We may collect the physical location of your device by using satellite, cell phone tower, or Wi-Fi signals. You may be permitted to allow or deny such uses of your device’s location, but if you do, we will not be able to provide you with the applicable personalized services and content.

  • App usage data

    When you download and use the App, we and our service providers may track and collect App usage data, such as the date and time the App on your device accesses our servers and what information and files have been downloaded to the App based on your device number.

  • Cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies

    Some of our automatic data collection is facilitated by cookies and similar technologies. Like many online services, we use the following technologies:

    Cookies, which are text files that websites store on a visitor’s device to uniquely identify the visitor’s browser or to store information or settings in the browser for the purpose of helping you navigate between pages efficiently, remembering your preferences, enabling functionality, helping us understand user activity and patterns, and facilitating analytics and online advertising.

    Pixel tags (also known as web beacons and clear GIFs), which may be used to demonstrate that a webpage or email was accessed or opened, or that certain content was viewed or clicked.

    We use Google Analytics, which uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze information about use of the Services and report on activities and trends. This service may also collect information regarding the use of other websites, apps and online resources. You can learn about Google’s practices by going to and exercising the opt-out provided by Google by downloading the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How Do We Use Your Personal Information?

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Services and fulfilling your requests

    • To provide the Services to you, such as by allowing you to submit reviews, assisting you with redeeming points for gift cards and other rewards, sending surveys to you, and providing you with related customer service
    • To allow you to participate in surveys from third parties
    • To respond to your inquiries and fulfill your requests, for example, when you send us questions, suggestions, compliments or complaints, or when you request information about our Services
    • To complete your transactions, verify your personal information, and provide you with related customer service
    • To send administrative information to you, such as changes to our terms, conditions, and policies

  • Analyzing personal information to improve the Services and providing personalized services

    • To analyze or predict our users’ preferences in order to prepare aggregated trend reports on how our digital content is used, so we can improve our Services
    • To better understand your interests and preferences, so that we can personalize our interactions with you and provide you with information and/or offers tailored to your interests
    • To determine which panels would be appropriate based on your interests, background, and experiences

  • Providing our newsletter and/or other marketing materials

    • To send you marketing related e-mails, with information about our Services and other news about our company

  • Allowing you to participate in sweepstakes, contests, and other promotions

    • We may offer you the opportunity to participate in a sweepstakes, contest, or other promotion.
    • Your personal information will be used to facilitate your participation in the sweepstakes, contest, or other promotion, including for receiving payouts and prizes.
    • Some of these promotions have additional rules about how we will use and disclose your personal information. Please read those additional rules before choosing to participate.

  • Aggregating and/or anonymizing personal information

    • We may aggregate and/or anonymize personal information so that it will no longer be considered personal information. We do so to generate other data for our use, which we may use and disclose for any purpose, as it no longer identifies you or any other individual.

  • For other business purposes

    • For audits, to verify that our internal processes function as intended and to address legal, regulatory, or contractual requirements
    • For fraud and security monitoring purposes, for example, to detect and prevent cyberattacks or attempts to commit identity theft
    • For enhancing, improving, repairing, maintaining, or modifying our current products and services
    • For developing new products and services

Your California privacy rights. As a California resident, you have the rights listed below. However, these rights are not absolute, and in certain cases we may decline your request as permitted by law.

    • You can request the following information about how we have collected and used your personal information during the past 12 months:

      • The business or commercial purpose for collecting and/or selling personal information
      • The categories of third parties with whom we share personal information

      • The categories of third parties to whom the personal information was sold or disclosed for a business purpose
    • You can also request a copy of the personal information that we have collected about you during the past 12 months.

  • Right to Non-discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
