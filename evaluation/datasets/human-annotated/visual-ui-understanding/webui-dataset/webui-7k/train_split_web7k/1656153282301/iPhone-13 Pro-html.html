F.A.Q.

What I need to use a Pankogut WordPress theme?

You need your own domain and a webspace with the last self-hosted wordpress.org installed.

How many times I can use a Pankogut WordPress theme?

You are free to use our themes on as many websites as you like. Pankogut WordPress themes have no restrictions on how many times you can download or use them.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes, if you are not satisfied with your theme purchase we offer a 15 days “no-question-asked” money back guarantee. Just write us a message to receive your money back.

Can I use your themes on WordPress.com?

At the moment you can’t use our themes at wordpress.com with a free plan, only with a Business Plan.

I already have a website, will your theme work for me?

Yes, as long as your host supports WordPress, our Themes would be a great way to redesign your current site.

Can I customize my theme?

Yes, our themes are published under the GNU General Public License, so you can customize them with no restrictions just as you like. You can find more information about the GNU free software license at GNU.org.

How can I get updates?

For each update you’ll receive an email with your download link.

What kind of support do you offer for your WordPress themes?

You can find answers to your dubts in our Support page where you can also ask support.

Can I upload video, images, add paypal buttons, etc.?

Yes, our Themes use WordPress, which allows you to add any type of media you want to your site.

Do you provide customizations?

We do not provide customization services. If you are looking for a custom design, we suggest hiring a trusted web agency like WP/TASK to assist you. Our themes can provide a great starting point for developers.
