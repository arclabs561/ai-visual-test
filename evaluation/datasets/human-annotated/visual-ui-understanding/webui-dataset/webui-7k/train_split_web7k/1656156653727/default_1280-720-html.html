Paperless Memberships

Here at the British Fantasy Society we understand that many of you like to read on the go, or have limited shelf space, and so don’t necessarily want hard copies of the BFS Journal, BFS Horizons and other publications – so we’ve gone paperless!

Quicker. Easier. Cheaper. But the same amount of awesome content!

You’ll have access to our publications by digital download suitable for most e-readers and tablets, and still get all the normal benefits of being a BFS Member for only £20; though please note that this digital membership option is single membership only. If you prefer to receive hard copies or require joint membership, please see the standard membership details.

Please provide your details in the fields below and complete the transaction via the PayPal “Buy Now” button. Please Note: We process new memberships and send out welcome emails once a month, so don’t worry if you don’t hear back from us immediately.
