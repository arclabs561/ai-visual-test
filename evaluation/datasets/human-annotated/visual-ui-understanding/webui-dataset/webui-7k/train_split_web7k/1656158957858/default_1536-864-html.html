16 November 2021

Fiddler’s Green Peculiar Parish Magazine is delighted to announce the publication of our eighth issue. Subtitled “Idyl Hearts,” Fiddler’s Green 8 is our largest to date, featuring copper-foil titling, a cover by Sigurd Persson, and 56 pages of art and magic. We anticipate printing and mailing Fiddler’s Green 8 in early winter, but with strong pre-order support it could happen sooner. Details at fiddlersgreenzine.com.
