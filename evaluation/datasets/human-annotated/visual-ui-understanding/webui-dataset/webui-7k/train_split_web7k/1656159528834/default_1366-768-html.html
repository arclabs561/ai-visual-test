Programs

SMASH Academy

HIGH SCHOOL (9th - 12th)

Our 3 year, intensive and holistic STEM focused college prep program that empowers students to deepen their talents and pursue STEM careers. Each summer, scholars are immersed in tuition-free studies in partnership with leading universities throughout the country.

Learn More


SMASH Rising

SMASH Rising provides alumni access to cutting-edge tech companies where they learn problem-solving skills and build their network as they gain real-world working experience.

SMASH Rising will be virtual for 2021.

Learn More
Scholar Spotlight

Meet Caleb | SMASH Morehouse

Caleb's journey with SMASH began in 2017 as part of the inaugural cohort at Morehouse College in Atlanta, Georgia. SMASH has helped propel him out of his shell so he can achieve his STEM goals.
