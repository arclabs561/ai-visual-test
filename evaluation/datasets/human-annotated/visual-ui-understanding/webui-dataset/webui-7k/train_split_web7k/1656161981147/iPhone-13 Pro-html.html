The Delaware Beaches

An infographic that maps the geography and recreational activities of each Delaware beach. Colors and patterns make it easy for viewers to compare and contrast what each beach town has to offer. This 17x40" poster was created as wall art for beach houses or for the chamber of commerce. The pattern of each beach town can be used as a system in other materials such as beach towels, t-shirts, buttons, and stickers.
