Dr Perrone specializes in general and cosmetic dentistry to restore and enhance the dental health of your smile. We utilize the most advanced dental technologies to create the most esthetic smiles, speed up recovery, and aid in providing the most comfortable experience possible.

Marielaina Perrone DDS can beautifully restore chips, gaps, and stains on the teeth with cosmetic dental bonding. This tooth-colored material is durable, is applied to the affected tooth, then sculpted for the most natural cosmetic results. Cosmetic bonding is a tried-and-true dental procedure that can be very effective for minor teeth imperfections that may not require porcelain veneers or other treatments to achieve the desired results.
