Q: What is a fanlisting?
A: According to TAFL, a fanlisting is: A fanlisting is a web clique that lists fans of a particular subject. Unlike most web cliques, a person does not need a website in order to join. Fans from around the world submit their information to their approved fanlisting and they are then listed to show their love for the subject.
