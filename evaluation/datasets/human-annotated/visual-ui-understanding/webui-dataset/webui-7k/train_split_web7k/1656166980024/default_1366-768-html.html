#144 | Articuno | フリーザー | Freezer

“Articuno is a legendary bird Pokémon that can control ice. The flapping of its wings chills the air. As a result, it is said that when this Pokémon flies, snow will fall.”

Snow will fall.

Welcome to Whitenoise Snowfall - the TFL approved fanlisting for Articuno. If you're a fan, please take a moment to add your name to the list!

Not sure what a fanlisting is? A fanlisting is simply an online list of fans of a subject, such as a TV show, actor, or musician, that is created by an individual and open for fans from around the world to join. There are no costs, and the only requirements to join a fanlisting are your name and country.
