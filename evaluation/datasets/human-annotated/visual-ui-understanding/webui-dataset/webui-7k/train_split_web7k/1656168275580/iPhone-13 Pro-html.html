Differentiated Thyroid Cancer

This special collection of recent publications regarding Differentiated Thyroid Cancer (DTC) from the American Thyroid Association® journals highlights current understanding and the most recently published information on this topic. The selected original studies, reviews, commentaries, and videos include content related to the diagnosis and management of DTC. This collection is geared for all clinicians who take care of patients with both uncomplicated and more complex cases of DTC.

We invite you to read these articles, commentaries, and videos, selected by the Editors-in-Chief of
Thyroid®, ClinicalThyroidology®, and VideoEndocrinology™.
