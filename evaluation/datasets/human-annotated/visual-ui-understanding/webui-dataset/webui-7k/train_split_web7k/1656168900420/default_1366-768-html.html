Submission form

Thank you for helping us record the past and present of the English language.

Find out what makes a good submission here, or fill out the form below to submit your new word or evidence to the OED. If you are contributing to our latest appeal, please submit your word via the relevant appeals page.

Submit words and evidence to the OED

  • Please state the part of speech, if known
  • As defined in the OED Online entry. e.g. 2.a.
  • Please add any information about the pronunciation of this word. You could use IPA, tell us what it rhymes with, or link to a recording of you or someone else saying it correctly.
  • What does this word or sense mean?
  • Please provide the author, the title of the book, its date of publication, the chapter number (if there is one), and the page number.
  • Please provide the name of the publication, the date and year of issue, the volume and issue numbers (if applicable), and the page number.
  • Please give us the URL at which you found the quotation.
  • Please tell us where you found the quotation.
  • Please add any supporting information or quotations, if you feel this would be helpful.
