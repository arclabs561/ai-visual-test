Current Campaigns

Tenant & Homeowner Association

The Tenant and Homeowner Association (THA) is led by low-income tenants and homeowners living in communities of color, who are facing eviction, foreclosure, and unsafe housing conditions.

Behind The Walls

The Behind the Walls Committee is a community of people who have been impacted by incarceration working in resistance to the prison system.

Community Safety Act

After passage of the Community Safety Act (CSA), we are pressuring the city to enforce the CSA and support the effective functioning of the Providence External Review Authority (PERA).
