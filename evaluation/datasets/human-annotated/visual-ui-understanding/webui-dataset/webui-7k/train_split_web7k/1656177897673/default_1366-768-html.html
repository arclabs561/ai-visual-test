In our time we face complex, interconnected challenges that span every aspect of human experience: the objects in our homes and the services we rely on; the stories and communities that shape who we are; the state of our nations and our environment; and the fast-moving evolution of our own species.

To address these challenges
we need a new kind of response
– a creative collective.

kyu has risen to embrace the challenge of its time – recruiting and enabling cutting edge creative organizations to go further and faster in solving the world’s biggest, most complex problems.

We’re building an inspiring portfolio of companies designed to understand, connect, and explore the edges of what our societies need now and what they’ll need in the decades to come.
