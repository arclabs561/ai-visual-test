    2. March 31, 2022

    Sekai Project Announces Entry Into The Cafe Business for Cat-Like Visual Novels

    Here at Sekai Project, there’s always a distinct hunger for the mysterious, the emotional, and of course, the moments that cause you to FREEZE in place. We are proud to announce on this day, March 31st, our new ventures into the café business, starting with frozen treats, as part of our efforts towards enhanced cat-like visual novel experiences for multiple locations around the contiguous United States.
