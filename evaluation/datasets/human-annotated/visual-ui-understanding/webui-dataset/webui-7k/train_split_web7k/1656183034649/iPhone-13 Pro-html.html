Fonts & Licensing

The primary font in use at the moment is Heliotrope, a beautiful font by Matthew Butterick (author of Practical Typography). It lies somewhere in between sans and serif and I love it. In the past, I've also used Concourse, Triplicate, and Valkyrie. Unfortunately, they're all proprietary fonts and can't be distributed in this repository; if you'd like to use them, you'll need to purchase them from MBType.com.

Everything else about the site was hand-created by me and is distributed under the MIT license.
