Northern Development, Mines, Natural Resources and Forestry (NRF)

Note: Following the June 18th cabinet shuffle, Northern Development and Mines (NDM) and Natural Resources and Forestry (NRF) have merged to become the Ministry of Northern Development, Mines, Natural Resources and Forestry (NDMNRF) The purpose of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry is to provide opportunities for resource development and outdoor recreation for the continuous economic and social benefit of the people of Ontario; and to administer, protect, and conserve public lands and waters. The ministry's programs are concerned with the use of the physical resources such as land, water, trees, fish, animals and certain minerals for resource utilization and recreation.
