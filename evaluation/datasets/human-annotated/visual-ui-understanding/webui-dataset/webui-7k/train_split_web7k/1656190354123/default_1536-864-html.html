OUTSIDER ART FAIR New York 2022

The gallery is delighted to debut at the 30th Anniversary of the Outsider Art Fair, visionary artist Peter Eglington from New South Wales, Australia. We are presenting four important paintings recently included in the Secret Life of Earth exhibit at the American Visionary Art Museum in 2020/21. Returning to OAF NY 2022 is geometric colorist, Helen Matteson, and psychedelic object painter, Rick Skogberg.

Proof of up to date vaccination (booster shot) is required for all those eligible upon entry to the fair. Guests are required to wear face masks at all times.
