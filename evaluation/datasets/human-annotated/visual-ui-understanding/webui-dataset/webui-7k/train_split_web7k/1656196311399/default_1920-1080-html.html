SPONSORSHIP OPPORTUNITES

  

Each February, for 37 years, we have hosted Race to the Sky sled dog races and also commemorate Camp Rimini War Dog Reception and Training Center near Helena Montana, for its efforts during World War II as a sled dog training facility. As an Iditarod Qualifier and part of the Rocky Mountain Triple Crown, we provide excellent veterinary care for the racing dogs. Our mission is to promote humane treatment of sled dogs, preserve and commemorate the historical and traditional use of sled dogs. We promote teamwork and have an active presence in schools.  There are Partnership Opportunities in several categories.  Come join us in this Historic Montana Tradition.
