  • Question 1

    Interpret the pressure measurement displayed by this gauge mechanism, assuming a gauge accuracy of 1

    How low and how high could this pressure actually be, given the stated accuracy of this gauge?
    Reveal answer

    Pressure = 3750 PSI 60 PSI

    This means the actual pressure could be as low as 3690 PSI or as high as 3810 PSI.

  • Question 4

    Interpret the pressure measurement displayed by this gauge mechanism, assuming a gauge accuracy of 2

    How low and how high could this pressure actually be, given the stated accuracy of this gauge?
    Reveal answer

    Pressure = 2.5 “HO 0.6 “HO. This means the actual pressure could be as low as 1.9 “HO or as high as 3.1 “HO.

    If you look closely at the photograph, you can see that the camera’s angle to the gauge face is not straight-on, and therefore there will be some in reading this gauge’s face. If we were to lower the camera’s view to get a more direct look at the gauge, we might see the needle pointing between the 2.5 and 3 divisions, which would mean a pressure of 2.75 “HO 0.6 “HO.

  • Question 7

    Interpret the temperature measurement displayed by this gauge mechanism, and also identify the meaning of the pointer:
    Reveal answer

    The current temperature is 40 degrees Celsius (red pointer), and the other pointer is a low-temperature capture. In this case, the ``capture’’ pointer shows that the temperature went down as low as 20 degrees Celsius (or perhaps a bit lower, since parallax error is making that pointer appear to read higher than it actually is).

  • Question 9

    Interpret the air flow measurement displayed by this rotameter:
    Reveal answer

    Rounding to the nearest half-division, we see the center of the ball-plummet nearest the ``1’’ mark. No units of measurement are specified on the scale (from this viewing angle, anyway), and so we cannot say if this is 1 SCFH, 1 SCFM, or anything else.
