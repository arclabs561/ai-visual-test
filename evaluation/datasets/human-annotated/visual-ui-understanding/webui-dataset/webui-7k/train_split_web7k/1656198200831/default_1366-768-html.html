Welcome!

McClatchy’s strives to be an employer of choice, and our benefits package is made with this goal in mind. With a focus on health, well-being, wealth and daily life, options include healthcare coverage for employees and their families, financial protection from expected and unexpected expenses, wellness resources and even coverage for four-legged friends.
