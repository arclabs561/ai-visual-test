Working Futures

A scenario planning game about the future of work

Requirements

<30
1-20
12+

Description

Artificial intelligence. Virtual reality. Genetic engineering. These and other technologies are changing the way we live—and the way we work. What will our jobs be like in a future marked by such radical change?

Working Futures is a card game that helps you explore possible scenarios for the future of work. By selecting various technological, political, economic, and cultural driving forces, you'll paint pictures of potential futures—then flip them on their heads!

We've used scenarios developed with this game to generate 14 short stories by science fiction authors that are now available in our Working Futures anthology collection. Learn more and order the book at workingfutur.es!
