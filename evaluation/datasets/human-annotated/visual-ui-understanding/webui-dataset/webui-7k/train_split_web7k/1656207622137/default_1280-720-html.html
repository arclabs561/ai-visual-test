About

Use the human voice is a small creative consultancy, founded and run by me, Giles Turnbull.

I work with companies, governments and non-profits to help them communicate about their work, as it happens. I advise on external comms, internal comms, web content and all other aspects of human-centered communication.

With over 25 years’ professional experience, I’m comfortable working alongside the most senior leaders and the most junior beginners. I provide training, advice and practical support.

My work is founded in the belief that corporate entities do not have to sound corporate in order to be understood. In fact, the more effort they make to sound approachable and human, the more clearly they’ll get their message across.

I can help with:

  • simplifying and clarifying your message
  • advising leaders on strategy and creative direction
  • generating creative ideas
  • building and coaching creative teams
  • filmmaking and presentation design
  • presentation technique and confidence-building
  • writing and editing
  • making it all sound like something people would actually say

Services

Implementing agile comms

Writing, editing, presentations, video: I can help you and your team apply agile thinking to creative output. Useful for internal and external communication.

Content audit

A detailed breakdown of the content you’ve got right now, and a list of suggested changes and improvements. A good starting point.

Content prototyping

What could your content be like? How different could it be? Let’s quickly build a web prototype to explore some options.

1-to-1 writing or blogging coaching

Regular conversation to help you improve your writing and editing skills. Weekly or fortnightly 1-hour sessions to suit your calendar, for as many weeks as we need.

Creative communication brainstorming

Working with your team to shape new ideas for overcoming stubborn communication challenges.

Walk it through

You, me, our walking boots and a day outdoors. Whatever your communication problems, let’s get outside and dig into them. Our goal for the day: clarity.

Training courses

  • Doing presentations
  • Writing for people who hate writing
  • … or something bespoke to meet your needs
