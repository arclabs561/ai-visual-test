MyIU

Alumni Directory

Looking for someone? Tap the IU alumni network

Members of the IU Alumni Association receive access to a database of nearly 750,000 IU alumni. Use the directory to make connections with alumni living in your city, update your personal information, or reconnect with old friends.

To search the directory, sign in or become an IUAA member.

Are you an IUAA member without a My IU login? It’s easy to create an account now.

Directory Use Policy »

Who has access to the Alumni Directory?

  • IUAA members receive access to search for friends and make connections.
  • All IU alumni who create a My IU account can update their alumni record in the directory.
  • IU faculty, staff, and current students may access the directory after creating an account.
  • IUAA is working with an external company, PCI, to produce a printed alumni directory. For more information, visit our FAQ page.
