Beijing travelogue

We had four days in Beijing in 2005, on our way back from Mongolia.

  1. the train from Mongolia
  2. the Beijing Hotel Shatan
  3. Jingshan Park + Donganmen night market
  4. the Forbidden City
  5. Zhongshan garden, Tiananmen Square, National Museum
  6. the Summer Palace
  7. National Art Museum, SE Watchtower, Temple of Heaven
  8. street life - Beijing old and new
  9. Lama Temple + Confucius Temple
  • what we didn't see
July is not the best time to visit Beijing. It was hot, muggy and hazy.
