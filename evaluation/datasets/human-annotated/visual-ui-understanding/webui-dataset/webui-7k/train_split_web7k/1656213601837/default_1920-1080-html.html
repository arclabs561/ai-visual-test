How can I get rid of all advertising? Print

Modified on: Fri, 13 Apr, 2018 at 3:56 PM

Advertising is one of the ways companies pay for the services they provide online. Opting out of ads completely is not possible under the AdChoices program. However, you can opt out of certain types of advertising. For example, some people prefer not to receive ads tailored to their interests based on their web browsing behavior. 


To opt out of interest-based advertising, you can visit opt-out pages like the Digital Advertising Alliance's WebChoices or AppChoices page. Note that you will continue to receive ads when you go online, but they will not be tailored to your interests.
