Convene

Click a link and you’re in. Ephemeral, lightweight, encrypted group chat in a web browser.

Learn more
Easy to Join

Click a link and you’re in a secure chat. No app or account required. No personal information requested.

A Safe Place to Connect

Convene offers an end-to-end encrypted conversation that safeguards against physical and digital threats.

Rooms that Self Destruct

Convene makes it easy to delete all message history and close a room.

Case study: Event hosts

Organizations can use this model to host temporary trainings or events with groups of any size. Check out our free, public version of this service at LetsConvene.im.

Keanu circle

Circle

Protect yourself with a circle of support. A tight-knit experience to check-in and share your location with people you trust.

Learn more
A Tight-Knit Experience

Your circle can check-in with each other, provide support, and share updates all from one spot..

Secure Location Sharing

Friends in your circle receive location updates through the app’s secure Matrix-based platform.

No History

What you see in the app is what you get. Nothing more is stored on servers.

Case Study: Women Journalists

Women journalists in Mexico use Circulo to support each other as they face online harassment and violence in their work. Check out encirculo.org.
