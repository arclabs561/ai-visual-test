Hey folks, it's been a while. Hope you're all doing well. 

This is the last update I'll be making here, as Nocturne is ending it's run at this point.


I know this may seem sudden, but comic making was already slow and difficult since early 2019, when I started working full time on animation and Nocturne had to take a backseat, going down to one page per week. 2020 has been rough for a lot of people, and despite transitioning to working from home, I still found I had way less free time and energy than I used to. Things snowballed into me feeling exhausted and no longer excited about working on the story.

I still love a lot of what I've done, and I'm happy with how much I grew as an artist as I worked on the comic. I'm grateful to everyone who read and commented on the pages, to every single fanart and word of excitement.

This is not the end the story and the characters deserve, but it's just how things turned out. Again, if you're reading this, I appreciate you and will work to properly see things through to the end on my next project. 

I'm sorry, and thank you for reading.


- Fred
