Extended deadline - Join the Chemistry Competition 2022

Are you interested in organizing your own Chemistry workshop? Then join our competition! The deadline to send us your proposal is extended until 30 September 2022.  

Especially for tenure trackers and post-doctoral scientists, the Lorentz Center organizes a Chemistry workshop competition.

The winning proposal is on a cutting-edge topic from any area of chemistry research. We encourage topics that bring together researchers from the academic community and the public/private sector.

Your workshop will be supported by and organized at the Lorentz Center, Leiden, the Netherlands.


How to join
See the call webpage for more information about the procedure and how to join.
