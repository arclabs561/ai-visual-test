About the owner

Name: Crissy
Fanlistings history:
  • 2003: Started joining fanlistings and building my own a bit later. I didn't own a domain and was hosted by various free hosts.
  • 2004-2005: I created my first collective, still hosted by others, using PHPFanBase as many others at the time.
  • 2007: Got my first domain. Some time between 2005 and this moment, I had made the switch to Enth.
  • 2008: Joined the TFL.org category staff as a Troubles staffer for the Musicians: Male category. Along the years I would also staff for Websites (Full Category), Animation (Finished/Updated/Closed), TV/Movie/Book Miscellany (Full Category) and TV Shows (Full Category).
  • 2010: Joined the Senior Staff.
  • 2013: After many years of being lucky enough to be hosted by friends, I become self-hosted and never looked back.
  • 2016: After 6 years in the Senior Staff and 8 as a category staffer, I decided to leave both positions for various reasons.
