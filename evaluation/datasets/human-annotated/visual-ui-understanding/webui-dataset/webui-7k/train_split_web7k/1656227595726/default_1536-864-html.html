Noname Book Club

By Emily Jackson  –  February 22, 2020  – 

Noname? No problem. Dallas Public Library is now part of Noname Book Club!

Launched in the summer of 2019 by Chicago rapper Noname, the book club picks two titles a month to discuss both online and in-person in discussion groups around the country. Described as “reading material for the homies”, Noname Book Club highlights books that speak on human conditions in critical and original ways while encouraging members to support the works of authors of color.

In addition to reading more books by authors of color, Noname also promote using local book resources like libraries and local book stores. You can find Noname Book Club tiles at DPL or order them through one of Dallas’s many independent book stores!

Each month DPL hosts a local meeting of the Noname Book Club! Find dates and more information on the event calendar.
