Welcome to Clarion UCSD's Twelfth Annual Write-a-Thon! What is a write-a-thon, anyway? Think charity walk-a-thon, where volunteers walk as far as they can in return for pledges. In the Write-a-Thon, our volunteers write instead of walking. Sponsors make donations or pledges to show support for the writer and the Clarion Science Fiction & Fantasy Writers' Workshop at UC San Diego.

After a two year delay, our Ghost class will finally be attending the UCSD workshop this year. We are so excited for them, and we need your help more than ever to make up for lost time and tuition. Your participation will help us rebuild, so we can continue to support our writers and Clarion's mission.

All donations are made through The Clarion Foundation, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, EIN #20-3114945.
