Field Inspections

As inspection management software, Fluix lets your field team easily collect data on mobile devices and generate comprehensive reports in line with customer requirements. Fluix can be used as an inspection software for pretty much any kind of digital field inspection:

  • Safety inspections, to predict safety hazards and guarantee your team stays safe in the workplace
  • Quality inspections, to ensure consistent compliance with industry regulations and professional standards
  • Preventive maintenance inspections, to avoid unanticipated issues that could result in costly downtime
  • Asset management inspections, to maximise the value and lifespan of your fleet.
  • Audit & Compliance inspections, to reduce the risk of non-compliance and ensure you’re always prepared for both scheduled audits and unannounced inspections.
