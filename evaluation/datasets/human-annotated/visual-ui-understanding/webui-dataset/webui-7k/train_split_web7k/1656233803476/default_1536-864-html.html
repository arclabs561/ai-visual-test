What does it mean to be an Agent?

The first and simplest method is to become a MagicSpam Agent. In this case, all you do is sign up and redirect traffic to our store front. You’ll have a special link for MagicSpam purchases directly on your site, and you’ll receive a percentage of all sales purchased through it; it's that simple! You will be sent cheques quarterly including funds not only from initial purchases, but recurring revenue as well.

What does it mean to be an Resller?

For those who wish to be more involved in the buying process, the Reseller program may be for you. Resellers purchase licenses through our Reseller License Manager at discounted rates depending on the volume of sales. We offer Bronze, Silver and Gold partnering programs. Gold partners even get access to our API's to allow purchases directly from your store application. You pay monthly for your licenses, and you can manage, suspend, or add licenses at your convenience with complete financial tracking. Silver and Gold members get special incentives including demo licenses, variable billing methods and more.
