Adcubum

We are a leading software manufacturer for the international insurance industry. The standard software Adcubum Syrius is our core product, a flexible, modular, cloud-ready system for health, property and casualty insurers. Working closely with our customers, we develop solutions for the insurance market of the future and create an ideal environment for agile work processes.

Our Solution

The flexible standard solution: Adcubum Syrius

Custom specification instead of do-it-yourself programming: Our insurance software combines the advantages of customisation with standard software and its lower costs.

Your sector

Health insurance

The Swiss health insurance market is characterised by fierce predatory competition and increasing regulation. The operative challenges include a cyclic end-of-year business and increasing customer demand for digitalised processes, comprehensive services and short processing times.

Find out more

Motor insurance

Motor insurance is the business line with the highest premium income within property and casualty insurance. This dynamic business is marked by competitive prices and shrinking margins that also cause problems for the fleet business, among others. At the same time, motor insurance business is crucial for customer acquisition and loyalty.

Find out more

Property insurance

Insurance companies in Germany are especially familiar with this problem: for years now, the margins for homeowners' insurance and commercial and industrial property insurance have come under a great deal of pressure. Increasing numbers of natural hazard claims and risks from mains water or storms/hail are burdening the business.

Learn more

Liability insurance

Private and commercial liability insurance are still generally considered a high-margin business. But the competition is increasing in this area as well: covers are being raised and premiums are being reduced.

Learn more

Casualty insurance

With casualty insurance, insurers have to generate the required efficiency benefits if they want to offer a highly competitive product with sustainable, healthy margins.

Learn more

Legal expense insurance

To achieve the required margins in private and commercial legal expenses insurance in Germany, insurers increasingly have to automate administration and claims processes and use service networks to attempt to limit claims frequency and settlements.

Learn more
