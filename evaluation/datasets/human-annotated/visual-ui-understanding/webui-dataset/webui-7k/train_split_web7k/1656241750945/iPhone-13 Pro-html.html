Privacy for Members

In order to become a registered member, you need to provide a username, and an email address. The email address is not publicly displayed anywhere, and not shared with any third parties. Emails are only sent as the result of actions on the site (e.g. requesting a new password), or disabled-by-default notification settings. We do not send any newsletters.


The IPs of registered members are also logged for the purpose of preventing spam and vote manipulation.


We do not build profiles on our users, besides storing the data you explicitly provide for features of the site (e.g. webcomic subscriptions), as well as IPs. We do not build a record of the pages you visit, what links you click, etc. Most actions that could result in personal data being stored in our database are triggered by pressing a button or link, which describes what you are about to do. ComicFury will always make a good faith effort to make it obvious what a form does when you submit it, and how it may result in data being stored and/or publicly displayed.


Personal data submitted to ComicFury is only used for the services ComicFury provides, it is not sold or given to other entities. However, most data is solicited for the purposes of publicly displaying it (e.g. comic pages, comments, forum posts, etc.).


Privacy for Non-members

Here are the ways ComicFury may interact with, or store your personal data, while browsing the site:

  • If you are not a member of this site, we will still temporarily log your IP address, in order to provide the unique visitor counter for webcomic authors. IPs saved for this purpose are automatically deleted after a few days, and are not used for other purposes.
  • If you leave a guest comment on a webcomic, we will also log an IP address along with your comment, to track and prevent abuse on the site. The contents of your comment are also publicly displayed (obviously).
  • When you first visit the site, the link you came from (the "Referer" header sent by your browser) will be stored in a cookie. This data is only permanently stored on the servers if you then sign up, to help us analyze where our members are coming from.
  • If you click a "save page" link on a webcomic, to set a bookmark, and you are not logged in, a guest session will be created, and a cookie will be set to associate you with that session. The session is only used for the purposes of providing the bookmark feature, and we do not build a profile or track your behaviour with it otherwise.
  • We also set a cookie to detect if your browser supports third-party cookies. This information is not stored on the server, and is used on webcomic sites for determining whether or not we can check if the user is logged in (through a cross-domain script inclusion), to hide the edit/delete links on comments the user is not authorized to edit/delete. To put it simply, it only has a cosmetic purpose and nothing else.
  • We store what forum threads were visited during this session in a cookie, to then mark these threads as read, on the forum display page.
