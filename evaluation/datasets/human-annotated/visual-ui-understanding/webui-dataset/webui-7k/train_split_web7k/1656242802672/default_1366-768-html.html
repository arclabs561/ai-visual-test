We're hiring a Community Manager

Published 2 December 2021, by Mark Picken in Hiring.

Deadline for applications has passed.

If you submitted an application, we’ll email to let you know we’ve received it and confirm if you’ve been shortlisted.

Job description

We’re hiring a new Community Manager to join our growing, remote UK based team.

View the full job description

What’s involved

As a member of the team, you’ll:

  • work with community members to understand their needs,
  • encourage participation and contribution from community members,
  • work with colleagues to create community content, events and workshops,
  • increase the diversity of community members and their voices, and represent us in online spaces and forums,
  • work alongside the social media manager to market CDL’s services through our various channels,
  • provide admin support, such as setting dates and times for meetings.

Pay and holiday

  • Salary: £30,000 to £38,000 pro rata.
  • Annual training budget: £800 pro rata.
  • Holiday: 28 days annual leave + Bank holidays plus Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve (or for part-time, in proportion to days worked)

Location

Remote, with infrequent company gatherings

We’ll remove any personal information before it’s shared with the hiring team. This reduces the chances of bias. I don’t think we have any at CDL but that’s the thing with unconscious bias. It’s unconscious.

Deadline for applications

Send a brief cover letter and your CV to (jointheteam@contentdesign.london)[mailto:jointheteam@contentdesign.london] by 17:30 (GMT) Thursday the 23rd of December. 2021. Any applications received after this date will not be considered. Interviews will take place during the middle of January 2022.
