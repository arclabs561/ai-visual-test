Anne Tibbets

 

Anne Tibbets joined DMLA in 2021, and was previously at D4EO Literary Agency. She represents adult and young adult commercial genre, primarily thrillers, mysteries, science fiction, fantasy, horror, and historical women’s fiction. A screenwriter and published author, Anne lives in the Los Angeles area and has a BA in Theatre Arts from California State University, Fullerton.

Submissions:

Anne is open to queries.

Anne is open to queries via Query Manager at https://QueryManager.com/AnneTibbets and personal referral only:

  • Adult Thriller: Domestic and/or Investigative
  • Adult Mystery: Cozy, Amateur Investigator, and/or Police Procedural
  • Adult Horror: Psychological, Non-western monsters and settings
  • Women’s Fiction – Historical: sweeping, epic, romantic, and feminist
  • Romantic Suspense: International Thrillers and RomCom Adventures
  • YA Historical: sweeping, epic, and feminist
  • Diversity: BIPOC, LGBTQ+, ability

Do not send:

  • Erotica
  • Screenplays
  • Poetry
  • Short story collections
  • Previously published or self-published works
  • Picture, Chapter, or Middle Grade books

Anne is open to queries on Query Manager at https://QueryManager.com/AnneTibbets and by personal referral.

Response times for partial and full submissions average about 3 to 4 months.

*Please do not query Anne at the same time as any other DMLA agent*
