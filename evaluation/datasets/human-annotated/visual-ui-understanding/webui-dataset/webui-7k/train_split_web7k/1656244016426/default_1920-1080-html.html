1.5 Privacy Policy

PaperCall only collect information explicitly shared with us. That includes your email and name from your single-sign-on provider when you register so we can email you updates after you’ve submitted talks. Beyond that the only data we collect is the submissions you’ve explicitly created on our platform and choose to submit to conferences. Submissions can only be seen by you and the event organizers to whom you are submitting. These are kept confidential until accepted and published.

All of our information is stored, encrypted, inside of a Google Cloud datacenter. Information is kept indefinitely unless you decide to delete your account, in which case you can just email us (support@papercall.io)

Your information is shared with events that you explicitly submit to, as well as made public when you mark a talk as “public”. For corporate customers, we also let speakers opt-in to share information with their organization. No third parties collect personal information or have access to the information we have collected.

We do use cookies to track your logged in session, once logged out that cookie is destroyed.

Users are able to delete any information they want about themselves from our platform or choose ask us to terminate their account (termination is permanent).
