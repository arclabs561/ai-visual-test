USB 4 Faster, Stronger, and Now With Thunderbolt!
 USB 4 Announced by USB Promoter Group
by Ernest Lilley

If you're still wringing your hands over having to give up USB Micro connectors for Type C, you probably won't be thrilled to know that the current versions, 3.2/3.1 are about to be upgraded to version 4. Actually, that has nothing to do with connectors, because connector type and USB specification are separate, and there's no suggestion that the type C has had its day.  The changes from 3 to 4 are all good, taking data transfer rates up to meet internet download speeds at 40 GBPS, the power and protocols to run multiple displays, and most interestingly, an end to the Apple Thunderbolt v USB debate, with Thunderbolt added into USB.
