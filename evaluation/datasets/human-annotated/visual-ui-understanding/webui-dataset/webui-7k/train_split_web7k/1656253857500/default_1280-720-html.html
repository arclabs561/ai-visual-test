Benchmark Group, L.L.C. is a full service land surveying, civil engineering and landscape architecture business. Founded in August of 2003 by Dave Fazekas, P.L.S. and Murray McCullough, P.E., the Benchmark Group offers its valued clients a variety of land surveying and engineering services at a competitive cost. In the summer of 2004, Wes Wilkerson, A.S.L.A. joined the firm as a partner broadening the services provided by Benchmark Group to include all facets of landscape architecture. Likewise, Mr. Russell J. Rome, P.E. joined Benchmark Group, L.L.C. in February 2005 as the fourth and final partner expanding the firm's experience and project capabilities. With a combined experience of over 75 years, Dave, Murray, Wes, and Russ are committed to assisting with making your surveying, engineering and landscape architecture projects a success
