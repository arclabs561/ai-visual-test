Editing

Capricious is a quarterly speculative fiction magazine, founded and edited by Andi. The magazine’s first special issue – a double length collection of stories which include gender diverse pronouns – was published in January 2018, and is available for purchase in paperback and ebook formats.

Andi has also co-edited two volumes of New Zealand speculative fiction (Regeneration and A Foreign Country) as well as the fundraising anthology Tales for Canterbury, and was an editorial board member for the 30th issue of JAAM.
