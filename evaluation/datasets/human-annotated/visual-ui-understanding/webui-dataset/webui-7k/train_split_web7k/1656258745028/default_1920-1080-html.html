With a 40+ year reputation

The Metalworks Institute campus, situated just outside of Toronto, is the educational creation and natural extension of the renowned Metalworks Studios, home to some of the world’s most successful artists and music producers.

  • Our Diploma Programs are designed for those who wish to understand the complexities of the entertainment industry and build a successful career on a solid base of knowledge.
  • Students are prepared for leadership roles and are taught about lifelong learning through innovative instruction techniques and exceptional learning resources.
