About

Chris Vanjonack is an M.F.A. candidate at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, a reader for Ninth Letter and No Contact, and an occasional contributor to Denver Westword. ​He's also a former language arts teacher from Fort Collins, Colorado, where he co-founded Slamogadro, and wrote + performed in three wildly unprofessional theater productions for the Fort Collins Fringe Festival.

He is a recipient of the 2020-2021 AWP Intro Journals Award, and his fiction and creative non-fiction have appeared or are forthcoming in One Story, Barrelhouse, DIAGRAM, Electric Literature, The Rumpus, CRAFT Literary, and elsewhere.
