Accessibility statement

The FPS Chancellery of the Prime Minister is committed to making this website accessible, in accordance with the Law of 19 July 2018 on the accessibility of the websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies.

This accessibility statement applies to the website https://www.belgium.be

Compliance status

This website is compliant to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 level AA, as proven by the Anysurfer label it obtained.

Preparation of your statement

An external accessibility audit was carried out on 8 March 2021, involving an in-depth analysis.
A review is scheduled every two years.

 

Contact information

If you have any questions or comments about the accessibility of this website, you can contact:
Online Team
DG External Communication
FPS Chancellery of the Prime Minister
Email: webmaster@premier.fed.be

If you do not receive a response, or the response you receive is unsatisfactory, you can contact the Federal Ombudsman.

 

This statement was prepared on 4 December 2020.
It was last reviewed on 8 March 2021.
