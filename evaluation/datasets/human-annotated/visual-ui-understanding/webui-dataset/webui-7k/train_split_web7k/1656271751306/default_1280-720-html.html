  • Overview

    With a lineage dating back to the 1930s, Teledyne LeCroy Test Services (TLTS), a division of Teledyne Technologies, Inc., has a reputation for high-quality, cost-effective products and technical support services.

    We have provided equipment and analytical services for use in hostile environments, including temperatures greater than 550 degrees C and ocean depths of 7 kilometers.

    Our transducers are found in highly diverse applications such as on the Space Shuttle robotic arm, deep-sea oil-drilling risers, natural-gas storage tanks, automobile test vehicles and nuclear power plant valves.
