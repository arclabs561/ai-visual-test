about the site

The Tactics games are my favorite subseries in the entire Final Fantasy series; FFTA was my first FF game, and I have a lot of love for FFT. When I opened ivalice.org in 2013, I knew I wanted to make a clique to go with it to celebrate one of the best parts about the Ivalice games: job classes! Sure, job classes have been around since the very first Final Fantasy and in several other titles since (I'm partial to FFV, myself), but the Tactics games are where they really shine.

This layout features art of the Elementalist job from FFTA2; it was always one of my favorite jobs in the first game and I love this artwork, found via Creative Uncut. Job sprites come from the FF Wiki. The texture is by badwolvesrun and the background is by chamberten (defunct).
