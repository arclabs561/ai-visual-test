International Rights

Author Services, Inc. is a wholly unique literary agency.

We are not here simply to license the publishing rights for Mr. Hubbard’s works, ranging from book publication, motion picture and television rights, games and other multimedia titles to audiobooks, serializations and a diversity of collateral areas.

We are interested, as well, in working closely with international publishers in the marketing and publicity of his books. Anything we can do, every resource and assistance we can furnish, to ensure that this occurs is our prime responsibility and chief concern.

With three decades of experience and a stellar history in publishing books around the world, we are interested in seeing that publishers do very well selling or distributing the works of L. Ron Hubbard.

We assist in a variety of ways, furnishing innovative promotional and marketing ideas. We launch books and promote the releases by getting media in foreign countries with the objective of selling more books. We also use the social media platforms and other innovative online marketing tools to reach audiences via the Internet.

During Mr. Hubbard’s prodigious 56-year literary career, he published hundreds of works of fiction and non-fiction, including a number of memorable works of speculative literature spanning from suspense and western to science fiction and fantasy.

In the subject areas of social, cultural and humanitarian significance, his prolific literary output spanned from self-help to the treatment and rehabilitation of drugs, to education, moral conduct and business management.

Today, there are over 325 million copies of Mr. Hubbard’s works in circulation with a record 19 New York Times bestsellers on the record.

A question might be posed as to how could work of this magnitude and impact be accomplished by one author in the course of 80 years. The answer is simple. These are works that are as timely as they are timeless and good writing always sells!
