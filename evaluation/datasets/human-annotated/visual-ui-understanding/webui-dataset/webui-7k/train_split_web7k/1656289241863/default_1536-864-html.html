2015 Jan 15  Emercoin Reaches Peering Agreement with OpenNIC. 

OpenNIC offers users a non-national alternative to traditional Top-Level Domain registries.
Since 2015 emercoin distributed blockchain DNS TLD domain zones -  .bazar, .coin, .lib, and .emc will be resolved not only through emercoin-dns, but also via public OpenNIC servers.
