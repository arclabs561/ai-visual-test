An accounting firm that gives you an advantage–that’s who we are.

You want a trusted, third party resource; one who gives you advice built on highly valued accounting services. A firm who keeps a laser-like focus on delivering results for you and your company. Our 100+ year old CPA firm gives you the advantage of having experts advising you who know your business and know how to make it better, easier, and more profitable.

Reaching beyond building a solid group of professionals, you will find a commitment to community and our environment as well. Our Tuscaloosa staff works in an architectural structure that is a gold LEED (Leadership in Environmental and Energy Design) certified building. We believe that by giving our accounting professionals the best possible work environment, encouraging work-life balance, and offering stellar educational and professional growth opportunities, we’re giving you a staff of experts who will provide the highest quality work for you.
