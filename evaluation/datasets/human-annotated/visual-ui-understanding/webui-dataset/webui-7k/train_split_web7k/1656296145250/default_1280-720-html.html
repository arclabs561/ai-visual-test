ConJosé is over

ConJosé pickles its website! That's right, as of July 1, 2004, ConJosé has stopped updating its website and has moved it to an archive graciously provided by Fanac.org. Due to CGI limitations, it is possible that some functions of this website will no longer work, and for that we apologize in advance for being unable to fix it so that it will work. Thank you all for making this journey with us!

ConJosé releases its 2003 Financial Report.

Links to Business Meeting notes in RTF & PDF forms, as well as preliminary HTML notes, have been added to the Business Meeting page.

ConJosé is over and the majority of the email addresses are dead, but controls for the mailing lists linger on for the time being.
