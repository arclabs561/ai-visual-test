When she was six years old, Valora Keri was tired of being bullied. So she did what any other kid would do in her situation — She freed a demon in exchange for the power to fight back.

She might have accidentally killed her mother in the process.

Since then, she’s spent her life running from her past and battling her demons - both literal and figurative.

Now, a kindly couple has given her a ride away from the scene of her latest disaster. When they roll into the town of Springfield after a long day of driving, all they’re looking for is a quiet place to bed down for the night.

But with Val’s luck, things are never that simple...

 

If you love flawed heroes who never stop fighting, snarky sidekicks, and found family, you’ll love this thrilling new Urban Fantasy series.
