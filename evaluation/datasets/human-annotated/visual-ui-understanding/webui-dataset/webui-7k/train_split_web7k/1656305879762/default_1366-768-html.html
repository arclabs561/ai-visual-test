Welcome to La Luna Cafe

La Luna Cafe is a counter-service neighborhood restaurant from Your Neighborhood Restaurant Group in the former home of Portland's La Luna rock club. We serve brunch classics from our beloved Simpatica restaurant such as Eggs Benedict, The Logger (chicken-fried Pork Loin with country gravy), and Crêpes. We also offer an assortment of health-conscious sandwiches, salads, juices, smoothies, and bowls, as well as a full espresso bar, breakfast cocktails, and a grab-and-go case. We have been serving our food in the Central Eastside Industrial District since 2005, and we look forward to hosting you!
