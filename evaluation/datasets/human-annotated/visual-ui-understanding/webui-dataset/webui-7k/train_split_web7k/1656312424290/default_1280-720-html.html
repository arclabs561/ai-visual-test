BIO

Jillian Glikbarg has been teaching yoga, tantra, meditation and yoga nidra in the San Francisco Bay Area and beyond for well over a decade. She is an E-RYT 500 certified yoga teacher and director of the Bindu Yoga Teacher Training School. She is the co-founder of Her Festival, a bi-annual gathering for womyn of all ages and backgrounds to share in the feminine side of collection, collaboration, and celebration. She brings truth and humor to her blogs and writings. She was featured on Recovering Yogi, has led global meditations through OMNES, and has written for (several other publications). She is an initiate in the Sri Vidya lineage of the Himalayan Sages.
