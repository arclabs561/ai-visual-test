Design Strategy & Innovation

UX STRAT brings together experienced design and product professionals from around the world to share methodologies, innovations, and tools focused on strategic design and innovation. UX STRAT features carefully curated presentations and workshops by speakers from industry-leading companies. The single track format of the conference includes ample time for discussion and networking. Our theme for 2022 is "Meaningful Relationships" as we are striving to help designers, researchers, data scientists, and product professionals reach the next level in their careers by teaching, inspiring and connecting them with experienced peers in an amazing city.

Earlier, there were vaccination requirements to enter the conference venue, but at this time, there are no vaccination requirements.

Why Attend UX STRAT 2022

UX STRAT is the world's premier conference focusing exclusively on product design strategy and innovation. Tickets are available for in-person attendance or video playback. The virtual experiences we have investigated and experimented with are a poor substitute for in-person experience, so we will not offer a remote option this year, but we will be working on a more engaging hybrid experience for future events.
