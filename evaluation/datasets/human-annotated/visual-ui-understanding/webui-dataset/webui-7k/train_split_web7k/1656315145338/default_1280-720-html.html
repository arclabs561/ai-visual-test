Economic Development Organizations

St. Lawrence Corridor Economic Development Commission: Brockville is one of the 7 proud members of the St. Lawrence Corridor. They are focused on the attraction of new industrial and commercial employers to the region, increasing the quality and quantity of job opportunities for its residents, and with assisting existing businesses to expand.

Economic Developers Association of Canada

Economic Developers Council of Ontario: The province of Ontario offers outstanding opportunities in every area of economic development. EDCO assists members to recognize and develop these opportunities.

Ontario East Economic Development:The Ontario East Economic Development Commission promotes Eastern Ontario as a centre for investment and business growth.
