INFORMATION

Description

Your original videos for general vlogging, random, fun videos & art is great. It will federate & be moderated to be work appropriate with others. This is a private instance but any current member can make a request for you. Share.TUBE is for your original content only or other peoples' creative commons licensed work that you review in clips respectfully.

If you'd like share.tube to follow your peertube instance please request via artsy[dot]af[at]protonmail[[dot]]com _ Support: liberapay.com/glocal & patreon.com/glocal

MODERATION

Terms

Non-sexual NSFW art content must be posted accordingly. No [real] videos or photographs (aka news) depicting violence against people or animals. The world is filled with it & there are other instances that can cater to exposing death & violence. Thank you - Also, do not threaten or use hate speech language.

For the most part, this instance will be moderated for a safe for work environment.
