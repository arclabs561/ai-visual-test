Game Over for Slide To Play

It is with a heavy heart that we announce the end of Slide To Play.

The massive changes in both the mobile game industry and the online publishing world mean that we need to shut down Slide To Play. In reality this happened a while ago, but there was still hope that a custodian or buyer would pop in and take over.

But now it is time to put down the controller and turn off the lights. Thanks for many great years and many great memories!

Our final score is 10,747 total posts over 11 great years!

On to other things,
with much love, the past and present staff of Slide To Play
