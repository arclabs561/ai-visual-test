Jared Vegosen

Jared Vegosen

35 | Co-founder and CEO | DV Trading

Crain's Chicago Business 40 Uner 40 2016

Jared Vegosen built his Chicago trading business one square foot at a time, expanding his traders into more office space until he occupied the entire floor of a Loop office building. Then he took half of another floor, and now he's part owner of that DV Trading headquarters building.

This year he bought out the futures brokerage firm that initially sponsored DV Trading. The firm has grown to 150 employees in its Chicago HQ, and 75 in the Toronto office run by his partner, as well as other locations. In an industry where firms can blow up overnight, he has methodically assembled one that has survived recent consolidation. DV is "a very successful trading firm," says Hazem Dawani, CEO of Chicago-based OptionsCity Software. And Vegosen is a "super smart and nice guy" to boot.

By the time Vegosen was a sophomore at Northwestern University, he preferred living downtown and was making "meaningful" money, first as a tech recruiter, then as a commercial real estate agent, and finally as a trader, before dropping out of college. "Being smart was always cool in my life," he says. A penchant for playing tennis runs in his family—his father presided over the United States Tennis Association and his brother was a pro—and an old court adage describes his business outlook: Just keep hitting the ball back and don't make errors.

Quick hits

In his spare time: Likes to hang with family, golf, exercise, play tennis, do yoga.

Current inspirations: The book "The Compound Effect," by Darren Hardy, and musical "Hamilton."

To get a culture/sports fix: Goes to see U.S. Open tennis matches, Hawks games and PGA golf events.
