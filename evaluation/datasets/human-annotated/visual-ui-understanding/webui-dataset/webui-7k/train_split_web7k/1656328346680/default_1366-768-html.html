    The New York State Council on the Arts is dedicated to preserving and expanding the rich and diverse cultural resources that are and will become the heritage of New York’s citizens. 

    The Young People's Chorus of New York City, a NYSCA grantee through our Arts Education Program in performance at the 92nd St. Y. Photo by Stephanie Berger.

    Over 1,500 Organizations are supported by NYSCA grants

    In 2022, NYSCA awarded more than 3,000 direct grants

    View More
    More than $100 million

    NYSCA awarded more than $100 million in FY2022 to support the visual, performing, literary and media arts, arts education and more!

    View More

    Rioult Dance performs Pascal Rioult’s Views of the Fleeting World. In 2018, NYSCA awarded Rioult general operating support through our Dance Program, funding for public school residencies through our Arts Education Program, and support for new theater seating through our Facilities Program. Photo by Basil Childers.

    $126 Billion

    New York State’s arts sector generated $126 billion in 2020, the most recent year recorded, playing a vital role in both quality of life and economic development

    What We Fund

    Supports traditional cultural expression practiced by ethnic, regional and occupational groups

    Folk Arts

    Supports capital projects improving accessibility, stability and sustainability of arts facilities

    Capital Projects

    Supports creative expression in all genres of time-based and moving image media, including emergent technology

    Electronic Media and Film

    Supports dance in all its diversity and stylistic breadth as well as residencies and rehearsal space

    Dance

    Supports in-school and community-based arts education programs for learners of all ages

    Arts Education

    Supports organizations devoted to architecture, historic preservation, fashion, graphic, industrial and/or interior design

    Architecture + Design

    Supports diverse arts and cultural activities emphasizing the exhibition and interpretation of art, objects or historic spaces

    Museum

    Supports contemporary art exhibitions, installations, workspace facilities, residencies, publications and critical writing

    Visual Arts

    Supports theatre in all forms, including classic, contemporary and musical theatre and experimentation that crosses disciplinary boundaries

    Theatre

    Supports arts activities that ensure access for historically underserved communities across a range of disciplines

    Special Arts Services

    Supports local arts funding, including partnership support with regional arts councils through our Statewide Community Regrant program

    State & Local Partnerships

    Supports presenters of live professional performing arts, including programming in single or multiple disciplines

    Presenting

    Supports organizations producing chamber music, choral music, early music, jazz, new music, opera and orchestral music as well as community music schools

    Music

    Supports the creation, publication, and dissemination of fiction, poetry, drama and literary prose

    Literature

    Supports new work by composers, theatre artists and film and media makers
