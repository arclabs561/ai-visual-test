Mid-Year Update
Hey there chaos people. I've had a busy few months, and updating my site hasn't been the highest priority. I'm working more than I ever have before, and keeping myself busy with more relaxing pursuits in my off time.

However, that's not to say I don't have any updates from the last few months. There are a few new illustrations in my gallery page! I participated in Creative Clash! I finally put the webring widget back on my homepage!

Most exciting of all, I have two collaborative zines available on itchio. One about rape and one about incest, each with a dozen or more super-talented contributors. I'm printing them as they're ordered, so if you haven't bought them yet, there's still time.

I'm planning to upload my short story zines to itchio soon as well. I'll send out an email once they're up.

No comic updates recently, but that's not to say I haven't been thinking about it. It'll get back on track one of these days.

Much love as always, stay safe.
