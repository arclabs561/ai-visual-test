At Social Blade, we’ve offered our services free to the world since 2008 and have been able to do that thanks to ads. At the start we were handling ads completely on our own through Google which worked, but after time ad rates started dropping. In 2015 we brought on a third party to manage our ads and revenue went way up! Over time though this ad provider was sold, and sold again and by 2019 ads weren’t so much their priority anymore and though our traffic was increasing substantially, ad rates had fallen so low we were in danger of not being able to pay for the site anymore. That’s when Next Millennium came along.

In November of 2019, right when advertisers typically have their largest ad budgets I got an email from Next Millennium quoting compared to what our current ad provider was doing an astronomically higher rate and decided to bite. Instead of replacing our current ad provider in full we set up a dual setup where Next Millennium would show three additional ads in addition to the four we were already showing. With only a fraction of all the ad slots filled they still were able to bring in more revenue than our current provider, often doubling or tripling it.

Now that we’re in 2020 which has been.. we’ll go with “interesting” year in terms of the economy and therefore ad spend, we still do continue to run multiple ad providers on the site for redundancy and security, but I can say that Next Millennium still to this day continues to beat our other ad provider in terms of daily revenue every single day.

~ Social Blade
