Floor Cleaning & Waxing

Dirt and grit in high traffic areas can leave beautiful floors looking dull. If not cleaned on a regular basis, floors can become scratched, nicked, or damaged. Ensure the cleanliness of your home or work flooring with New York City floor waxing services. When you undergo quality NYC floor waxing and cleaning, your floors will maintain shine and appearance through daily wear and tear. Hiring New York City professional floor cleaning businesses can increase the longevity of your floor and save you money long-term.

New York City floor waxing services offer top-notch floor cleaning services for all types of floor surfaces. Types of floors that can be cleaned by a NYC professional floor cleaning business include vinyl, tile, marble, ceramic, and hardwood. Cement, slate, terra cotta, and no-wax floors can also be professionally cleaned. NYC floor waxing services not only maintain homes, but are often found in all types of businesses, small and large. Skilled professionals of NYC floor waxing businesses can be used to maintain busy apartment complexes, schools, hospitals, hotels, day cares, and other places where commercial janitorial services are required due to high traffic.

New York City professional floor cleaning services will exceed your expectations. Home and business owners can assure that they are receiving excellent janitorial floor cleaning and waxing. These NYC floor waxing services have all the best supplies to give your floor a clean appearance and like-new shine. New York City professional floor cleaning services also include the following:

  • Dust mopping to ensure that all loose dirt, sand, and grit has been removed from the floor and around the baseboards. All furniture is removed from the room prior to a professional cleaning to ensure a thorough cleaning.
  • If cleaning is performed in a business location, the NYC floor waxing business will set up Wet Floor signs to advise employees and the public against potential slipping hazards.
  • Floor stripping is prepared according to the manufacturer’s instructions and the floor is stripped once cleaned.
  • Stripping solution is applied to small sections of the floor at a time. The solution sets on the floor for approximately 5 to 10 minutes, depending on the condition of the floor and type of floor involved.
  • Many NYC floor waxing services use an auto-scrubber attached to an abrasive pad to remove old sealer on the floor. To remove a build-up of stripping solution, a mop or squeegee is used. Excess water and solution is then soaked up.
  • The floor is cleaned with a damp mop with clear rinse water. It is then allowed to thoroughly dry.
  • Floors are completed after applying a sealer of wax. The type of sealer will depend on the type of floor.

New York City floor waxing services offer top-notch floor cleaning services for all types of floor surfaces.

It’s a well-known fact that regular floor cleaning and waxing will help to extend the longevity of the floor’s surface and keep them looking their best. When you hire a New York City professional floor cleaning business, you can rest assure that you will get a team of reliable, experienced, and fully-trained professionals who are knowledgeable on the best floor cleaning products for the job, and can make your floors look like new. High-traffic areas can result in dull floors that appear prematurely aged. A NYC floor waxing business can restore the natural beauty of a floor.

Floors require special care and using the wrong products or tools can lead to discoloration and damage. The primary benefit of using a New York City professional floor cleaning business is the assurance that your floors will look nearly new without suffering any type of damage. Floors can also be sealed to prevent further damage from occurring from all types of wear and tear. The following services provided by NYC floor waxing service are highly effective in maintaining and improving the look of all floor types.

High-Speed Polishing: High-speed polishing machines give floors a professional look and remarkable shine.

Honing: Honing is the process of removing scratches and etching from marble and stone floors to create a smooth, shiny surface.

Grinding: Grinding is the method of removing adhesive, scratches, and similar products and minor damage using an industrial grinding machine.

Waxing and Sealing: Waxing and sealing products used by NYC floor waxing services protect floors with high-quality products.

Floor cleaning and waxing are a highly important part of home and business care. Dirt and grime can compromise the finish and appearance of many types of flooring. If left unattended, dirt and sand coupled with friction from foot traffic can lead to permanent scratches and nicks, which are not only unattractive but can also be a safety hazard. Floors can stay well maintained and always look professional with the assistance of a New York City floor waxing service.
