 Protecting the Rights & Interests of Domain Owners The ICA promotes and shares best practices among participants in the domain name industry and educates consumers, policy makers, law makers and the media about the value and benefits of domain name industry. Become a Member

The Internet Commerce Association (ICA) is a non-profit advocating for the rights and interests of domain name owners and related service providers.

Our board members are actively engaged in all areas of the domain name industry including domain law & litigation, policy, regulations, buying & selling, investing, and development.   We advocate for domain owners and work to improve the domain name industry.
Join Us in Ensuring a Bright Future for the Domain Industry

UDRP Case Summaries

Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy was established and adopted at ICANN’s inception as a dispute resolution policy to resolve disputes between trademark holders and domain owners more quickly and cost-effectively than judicial litigation.
