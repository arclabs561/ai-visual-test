#LondonTechShow

Information Technology colleagues, our annual event has gone virtual!

As you may be aware, our 2020 Technology Showcase event was postponed in April due to COVID. At that time, we rescheduled our onsite event for this September. Even though we are doing well in South Western Ontario with relatively small numbers of new cases, we acknowledge that jamming close to 300 people into a trade showroom is not cool. So, we’ve gone virtual!

On Tuesday, September 22nd, select technology vendors will present their offerings during online seminars and a virtual tradeshow. This will be our 16th annual event and the first to be delivered online. A virtual format is not our preferred choice but we still expect to provide compelling content that will help you navigate the constantly evolving technology and information security landscape.

Thank you for your ongoing support and we look forward to “seeing” you at this year’s event.

Regards,

Paul Jeneroux and Todd Cox
Attache Group Inc.
