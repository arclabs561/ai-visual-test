Join IASSW in 2022

Connect with the community of International Social Work and enjoy benefits of membership

Benefits of Membership:

  • Right to participate in governance of the association
  • Opportunities to apply and benefit from IASSW projects and funds
  • Eligibility to participate in the proceedings of the UN
  • Reduced fees for Biennial International Conference
  • Access to a worldwide network of social work educators
  • Participation in nominations for honouring social work educators
  • Free access to the IASSW website member area and Social Dialogue magazine
  • Free subscription to the International Social Work Journal (for individual members)
  • Free access to the recorded lectures, seminars or conferences hosted by IASSW (new in 2021)
