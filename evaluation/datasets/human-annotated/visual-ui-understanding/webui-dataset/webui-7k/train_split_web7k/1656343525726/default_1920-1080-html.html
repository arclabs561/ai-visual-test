The Brief

Gymshark’s purpose is to unite the conditioning community, those who give 100% today to be 1% better tomorrow.

How do we empower people to build a better future for themselves and overcome the barriers they face to create a healthier life?

FAQ
Can I enter more than once?
Yes. Remember quality beats quantity.
How should my team be set up?
Up to you. We ask for 5 team members maximum. All members will get credit if your team wins and a virtual pass to Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity, but only two team members will present live on the virtual stage.
Can I amend my entry?
Yes. Email your details and entry code at futurelions@akqa.com.
Is there an entry fee?
No, you’re starting out, keep your money.
