Hello, we're Kleinschmidt

We create the managed EDI and API integration solutions that power the supply chains of leading companies around the world.

Klein­schmidt provides true any-to-any EDI and API integration solutions that enable companies of all sizes to build seamless supply chains by integrating with any trading partner imaginable. Our mission is simple: We’re here to solve the tough integration problems that no one else can. 

We’ve been digitizing commerce through innovative technology for more than a century, and our data integration expertise is unrivaled. As EDI integration and supply chain connec­tivity solution pioneers, our robust and reliable EDI and API connec­tivity solutions have powered the supply chains of household brands for over 40 years by enabling growth, disrupting industries and opening doors to new opportunities.

Managed EDI

We do it all. A dedicated team manages your EDI and API integration, translation, and commu­ni­ca­tion, trading partner onboarding, and more. And because we’re cloud-based, your EDI program remains flexible and agile as it scales.

EDI & API Integration

We build powerful and agile EDI and API connections to your trading partner community, enabling seamless B2B messaging to and from nearly any enterprise application or business system.

FreightLaunch

Freight­Launch is an easy-to-use web application that closes supply chain gaps by enabling full trucking EDI compliance. Carriers of all sizes are able to send and receive a full slate of EDI documents without requiring complex integration. 

EDI & Data Translation

Our cloud-based EDI translation solution provides true any-to-any capability and eliminates the need for on-premise EDI translation software. We seamlessly transform data on the fly from X12 to XML to JSON — and more.

Customs & Trade Compliance

Our government-certified, integrated and hosted compliance solutions for U.S. and Canadian Customs enable you to meet CBP and CBSA mandates for ACE and ACI eManifest, AES, ISF, and more.

CLM & Rail

A customized Klein­schmidt Car Location Message solution simplifies the collection and utilization of your rail car fleet data by providing real-time insight and connections to Class I, II, and III railroads.
