Things to Remember:

  • Need to update your email or other registration information on your Hub account?  Go to your profile picture in the upper right-hand corner of your Hub homepage>click on the drop-down arrow to the right>choose 'profile'>click 'Edit profile'>change desired items and be sure to click 'Save'!
  • Signed up for a course and can no longer attend? Please go to How to Unenroll Yourself From a Course 
  • Renewal certificates! Be sure to download and save all course completion certificates to your computer directly upon completion of the course. We also suggest printing a hard copy to store in a safe place for easy reference when you need it! 
  • Please know that . . . . when you create an account on the Hub you agree to the Terms of Use. Please take a moment to read through these. They are the guidelines for participation in the Teacher Learning Hub. There are some things that are never allowed on the Hub and it is just good practice to be aware of the guidelines. We want everyone to always have a positive experience and be able to participate fully in this invaluable no-cost-to-you resource!
