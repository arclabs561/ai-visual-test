Bitcoin VPS - Pay for your VPS with Bitcoin

Deploy Now

Bitcoin, the internet of money - not money of the internet

The idea of programmable money is a rather foreign concept. The prospect of transferring digital value without a 3rd party intermediary is equally as unfamiliar.

The introduction of Bitcoin by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2008 made both of these use-cases a reality. Anybody with a connection to the open internet can now transact online without needing permission. It is a revolution of the modern financial system.

Programmers are familiar with "if x, do y". The internet of money, the internet of value, Bitcoin, allows us to say "if x, spend y", "if x, lock y". This opens up the possibility for an endless stream of applications that were not possible before the advent of Bitcoin, cryptocurrency and blockchain.

Benefits of a Bitcoin VPS

  • A crypto friendly VPS
  • No Credit/Debit card required
  • No PayPal account required
  • Automate VPS renewal with compatible BTC wallets
  • Support open ecosystems by using cryptocurrency

What can a Bitcoin VPS do?

The same things a regular VPS can do, of course! It is merely a more convenient method of obtaining hosting services without needing to make use of 3rd party payment providers.

Whether it's Linux or Windows VPS hosting you're looking for, you can pay with Bitcoin. Our clients love having this option.

According to our clients, some of them make use of their server as a literal Bitcoin VPS by hosting a Bitcoin full node on the server. Running a full node helps to decentralize the Bitcoin network as well as making it easier for new-comers to participate in the network.

Other clients use their server for hosting a Masternode VPS or for staking their various crypto coins. Masternode/staking nodes are becoming more popular in recent times. With the innovative new advances in Fintech and Defi, the future of finance is set to offer many possibilities to its users.

There are many other innovative uses people find for their Bitcoin VPS, and we are proud to be able to contribute to the entrepreneurial nature that open ecosystems like Bitcoin bring to the table.



How can I pick up some Bitcoin to pay for the VPS?

Be aware! There are a lot of scams out there. There are several ways you can lose your money if you're not careful. As we're sure you're aware, the value of Bitcoin fluctuates a lot, so we certainly are not recommending you invest in Bitcoin - this page should not be considered financial advice in any regard!

With that said, obtaining Bitcoin is quite straight forward if you don't have any. Simply head on over to Coinbase or any other reputable fiat to crypto provider, and they'll guide you through the process.

Once you actually own some BTC paying for the VPS is a breeze. After you've configured your order simply select Bitcoin as a payment method, and we'll give you the Bitcoin address to send your payment to. Or you can scan the QR code on the page and your transaction will be generated automatically. Doesn't get much easier than that, right?

A VPS isn't the only thing you can buy with Bitcoin at Evolution Host.

Many of our clients opt to pay for their game server hosting with Bitcoin over traditional fiat currencies. In particular, Minecraft server hosting is quite popular with our Bitcoin-paying-clientèle. With a wide variety of services to choose from, and a wide variety of cryptos to pay with - the digital world is in your hands.



When is the best time to buy a VPS with Bitcoin?

Our data shows that people do not like to spend their Bitcoin at the following times:

  • Bitcoin's value is increasing
  • Bitcoin's value is decreasing
We have observed this to be true time and time again. The rolling theory for this would be that, when the price of Bitcoin is going up, people do not want to lose out on the potential for further gains from the price continuing to grow. On the other hand, when the Bitcoin value is decreasing, people feel like they are spending more than they would have previously on their Bitcoin VPS.

So, according to our data, people have deemed the right time to buy a VPS with Bitcoin to be: When the value of Bitcoin is relatively stable. During the lull periods of Bitcoin's price action, people have a tendency to become familiar with the low-fluctuating price ranges and therefore feel more at ease parting ways with their hard-earned Satoshi's.
