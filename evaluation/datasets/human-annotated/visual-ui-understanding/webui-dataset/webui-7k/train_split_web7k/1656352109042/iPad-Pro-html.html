Ardisson

Ardisson (a.k.a. Charles Matthews) has built a reputation for giving crowds exactly what they didn’t know they wanted. He’s best known for the industrial techno out on Seed records, and his profound TB303 mastery such as this clip from 030303 Acid Party in Ultrecht 2007:

Lately Charles has been deep in research, augmenting gamelan music with generative patterns and algorithmic warping. However he promises to translate some of this work back to a future algorave, bringing a taste of Indonesia to aleatoric acid.
