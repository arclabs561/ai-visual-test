June 8, 2022

Update 6/9/22 5:05A: This maintenance is now complete

Tonight (6/8/22), starting at 11:59 PM, a subset of customers in the San Francisco area will experience a brief service interruption while we perform some maintenance. The expected down time is about 30 minutes while the equipment upgrades. The maintenance window will be 5 hours.
