Every online casino in the UK has terms and conditions, these are designed to protect both the casino and the players, and most gambling sites will also have wagering requirements. It is important to understand these wagering requirements if you want to determine whether or not an online casino is fair and if their promotions are really worth it.

The wagering requirements will help you decide if a site really is as good as it looks at first glance. Most free spins no deposit offers, welcome bonuses, no deposit bonuses, and other promotional offers have wagering requirements that you need to meet before you can cash out and claim your winnings. Otherwise, you could simply create an account and then withdraw the bonus again, which would be a terrible business model for any online company.

The specifics of the wagering requirement will differ from one casino to the next, but a wagering requirement is basically a multiplier that represents the number of times a player needs to wager (playthrough) before they can cash out and take their winnings.

  • For example: If a game has a wagering requirement of 10x and you win £10 it means that you have to wager £100 before you can withdraw your winnings. 10 x £10 = £100.

What is the Probability of Winning?

People enjoy online gambling for many different reasons. For some people, it is a bit of fun and a good way to relax after a long day at work. For others, it’s the allure of the flickering lights and the attraction of the life-changing jackpot win. Regardless of why people gamble, most want to win something. But what is the probability of beating the house when you play at an online casino site?

  • Gambling is all about math. The probability of winning at a casino site or game depends on the math.  If you want to find a game where the math is more in your favour, you need to check the RTP (return to player). This is the rate that the casino pays out to players. It is based on all bets placed, not just your bet. For example, a game with an RTP of 90% pays out over £90 for every £100 bet. This does not mean that every player gets a 90% return on every bet because the percentage payout is calculated over millions of spins or hands. To find out more about the math behind gambling read “A Model of Casino Gambling”.
  • Slot machines offer good payouts. Slots sites are popular for a reason and while the RTP of slot games will differ from one machine to the next, for the most part, it will be between 95% and 98%. This gives you a good chance of winning something, but it is only the progressive jackpot slots that payout life-changing amounts.
  • Slots can be volatile. The volatility of a slot machine refers to how much and how often the machine will payout. Low volatility slots payout low amounts frequently while high volatility slots payout high amounts infrequently. That means the higher the volatility the more you could win but the lower your chances of winning.
  • Table games typically have the best odds.  Ever wondered why Blackjack is so popular with experienced online gamblers. The answer is simple; it offers the best odds of paying out and the house edge is often close to 1%. Craps also has great odds with a house edge of approximately 1.2% and with Roulette, most casinos have a house edge of 2.7%. At 1%, Baccarat has the lowest house edge of any casino game, making it a favourite with high rollers and whales. The house edge is the built-in advantage that the casino has, i.e. the casinos average profit from a player’s bet.
  • The Wagering requirements of a casino game or site also affect the probability of winning. A game might look good at first glance but if the wagering requirements are high, you will have to wager a lot before you can claim your winnings, and this means the odds are not in your favour. It is important to check the wagering requirements before you claim a bonus or play a game.
