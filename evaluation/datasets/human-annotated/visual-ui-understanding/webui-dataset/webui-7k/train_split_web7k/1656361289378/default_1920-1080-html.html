Fact sheet

Calligraphy is the art of mastering the hand, so it expresses emotions that words by themselves cannot convey. It is not by mistake that poems, and poetry writing has always been accompanied by calligraphic compositions. Greeting cards, birthday cards, thank-you cards or invitations could not be conceived without at least a touch of formal writing, or elegant cursive. How could one create a wedding invitation without the help of a classic copperplate ornamented handwriting ? How could any menu be designed without the help of some chancery or uncial styled typography ? And even today, could one imagine the internet without the highly calligraphy italic a, surrounded by an arabesque, the ubiquitous @ ?
Calligraphic fonts are the most amazing pieces of typography. Although they have an unmistakable old fashion look, they always charm the eye, especially when used elegantly as initials in beautifully set text (see statement above), or for invitations, poems, affirmations, and proverbs. For the type designer, the art of calligraphy is the basis of all creation. Although today, most of our craft is done with a computer, one should never forget the origin of our written language. I generally start any type creation by calligraphy. Besides giving me an instant feel of the construction that would not be possible on the screen, the square pen always helps to find the best curves, where mathematics have less to do than relationship with the medium. As Lao Tzu says in the Tao Te Ching : "the virtuous takes everything for difficult, and finally does not encounter any difficulty". Taking time to draw glyph on paper ultimately saves times on the computer...

Calligraphy & Swash

on't be shy ! If you find swash that is exceptionally beautiful, set it extraordinarily large and use it as a graphic element on your page -- show if off ! Set it in a pale color or as a shade of gray and let it swoop under your body text or behind a photograph. Use huge swash character as initial cap or as a lead-in to an article or chapter. Combine one large italic swash character with heavy, bold, black sans serif characters for a striking contrast in a title or quote.
