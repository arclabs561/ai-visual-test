The Veteran Hub of Houston

The Combined Arms headquarters, located in East Downtown Houston, is a rally point where our member organizations, veterans community and supporters come together.

Our facility features space to connect, co-work and collaborate—as well as providing permanent workspace for 20+ member organizations. Click through the map below for an insider’s view.

GYM FACILITY

Our 3,000 square foot gym facility is open to all veterans and is where we host weekly boxing classes and other physical wellness programming. To learn more about our events at the gym, visit our events page.

Annex

Our Annex offers work space for veteran impact organizations and a lounge area for collaborative conversations and relaxation with fellow veterans. Does this look like the perfect place for you to co-work and build community? Send us a request.

Entrance

Our building is a beacon of welcome to Houston veterans and supporters. We host a variety of professional development, social and wellness programming here; you’ll love the community that you connect to inside our walls. Don’t forget to look up—you’ll occasionally see us connecting on our rooftop over BBQ and beer.

Cafe

Coffee and camaraderie, our cafe is always bustling. Stop by for a cup of Joe and stay for the community. If you are looking for a place to co-work, send us a request.

Lobby

Welcome to Combined Arms. Our friendly team is ready and able to connect you to the resources you need and the events that you’ll grow from. You can take your assessments in our office or get the process started online.

Office Space

Home to 13 veteran organizations, Combined Arms is proud to provide over $250,000 in rent and annual operating expenses to these orgs so they can focus on serving our veteran community. Want to join us? Connect with us here.

CONFERENCE ROOM

A high tech space perfect for trainings and community meetings, our conference room can hold up to XX attendees. From executive chats to Toastmaster’s and beyond, this is a great space available to the veteran community upon request. Connect us here with questions.
