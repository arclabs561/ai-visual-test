Monsters Outside

May 8, 2017 5 Comments

Share3
Tweet
Email
More
3 Shares

“Daddy, Daddy,” Chad yells out. The seven-year-old boy comes running up to his father, covered in goosebumps, his heart pounding.

“What’s the matter, Chad?” his father inquires.

“There’s monsters outside. They’re everywhere and they’re really scary.”

His father looks down at the imaginative child and decides to take a look for himself. When Chad’s father returns his whole body is shaking and his eyes are glazed with fear. In a high-pitched voice his father says, “We’ll leave right now, Son.”

They get in their vehicle. Chad feels better once they leave.

Chad looks down at planet Earth as the spaceship ascends.

By Denny E. Marshall
