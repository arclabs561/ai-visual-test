PLANET VO² IN A NUTSHELL

Planet VO² is software part of the Planet range, the leading digital solutions for demonstrator vehicles and used-car management. Planet VO² is a structured, open, collaborative software program built to meet the new challenges of car retailing.

BENEFITS

Our solution lets you manage your trade-ins, stock, sales and listings, as well as your teams and your business, all with the same tool. Planet VO² is designed to help you structure your business, boost your new and used-car sales and improve your margins.
