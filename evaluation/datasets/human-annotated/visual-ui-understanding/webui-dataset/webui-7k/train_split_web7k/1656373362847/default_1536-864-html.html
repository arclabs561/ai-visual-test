Sponsoring a Contra Dance

Celebrating a birthday?  Wedding? Anniversary?  Lifetime milestone?  Celebrating life? 
You can support - in part or whole - an upcoming MCDC contra dance.  
Levels of Patronage
Platinum - Donation: $573
(full patronage & free dance for all attendees)                        
Gold - Donation: $375
(partial patronage & $5 dance admission for all attendees)                
Silver - Donation: $300
(partial patronage & $6 dance admission for all attendees)                
Bronze - Donation: As able
(partial patronage & regular dance admission for all attendees)        
For more information, please see Craig Hemphill, Elizabeth Todd, or Laura Frank - or email or click here for this form (which you can fill out and bring to any MCDC dance event).
