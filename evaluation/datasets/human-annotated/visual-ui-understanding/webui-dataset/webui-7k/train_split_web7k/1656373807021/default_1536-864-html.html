Help for Refugees

As featured on CNN, Gothamist, and WNYC

We are a grassroots, volunteer effort dedicated to helping Ukraine.

Direct Help for Ukraine is run by American volunteers who hail from former Soviet republics. We are shocked and horrified by the war in Ukraine, and are trying to do all we can to help Ukrainians protect their country and democracy.
