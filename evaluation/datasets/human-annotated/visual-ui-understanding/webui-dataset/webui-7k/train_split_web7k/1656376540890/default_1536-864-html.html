Agile Manchester is joining with our other community events for our second Lean Agile Exchange virtual conference. LAx brings together Agile Manchester, Lean Agile Scotland, Agile Cambridge, Agile in the City: London and Agile in the City: Bristol.

The combined conference will include a mixture of inspiring speakers tutorials, engaging live talks, interactive group sessions, speaker Q&A - plus a hallway track where you can meet other participants.

Tickets are available now visit our website for more information.
