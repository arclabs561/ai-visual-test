Welcome to Wild-Seven.Org. Here you will find fansites revolving around Animation, Manga, Music, and Video Games. Previous domains where this collective was located include Bevelle.com, Heavenly.nu, Heartilly.com, Aftercolony.org, and FaintSmile.org. During high school, I discovered the online community and taught myself web design. After college, I rediscovered my interest in technology and art, and decide to rebuild what I once took solace in. This is the result.
