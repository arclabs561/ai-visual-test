ALE team (Inria/CWI)
Activities Objectives Publications Software Team

ALE stands for Agile Language Engineering! The team contributes to the field of Software Language Engineering, aiming to provide more agility to both language designers and language users. We explore extreme modularity constructs for the development of Domain-Specific Languages, and advanced mechanisms for live modeling.

ALE is a research associate team (2017-2022), between the DiverSE team at Inria (France) and the SWAT team at CWI (The Netherlands).
